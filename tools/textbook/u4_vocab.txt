1.	gate [get]（名詞）大門
(1)	指的是進去某大型場地或建築物時，要經過的「大門」。
例：Most people go into the school through the front    gate   .
（大部分的人從前門進入學校。）
一字多義
	乘坐飛機的旅客上下飛機時，需要經由「登機門；登機口」，也稱作gate。
例：We’ll get on the plane through    Gate    A7.（我們要從A7門上飛機。）

2.	file [faɪl]（名詞）檔案
(1)	指「檔案；文件夾」，可為紙本或電子檔案。
例：The company keeps a    file    on all workers.（公司持有全部員工的檔案。）

3.	quiz [kwɪz]（名詞）小考
(1)	指較不正式的「小考；小測驗」。
例：Junior high school students take lots of   quizzes   every day.
（國中生每天都有很多小考。）

4.	blackboard [ˋblæk͵bɔrd]（名詞）黑板
(1)	本字是由black＋board（板）組成，而「白板」則是whiteboard。和board相關的複合名詞還有cutting board（砧板）、board game（桌遊）等。
例：Our   blackboard   fell during an earthquake and broke in half.
（地震時我們的黑板掉落並裂成兩半。）

8.	button [ˋbʌtṇ]（名詞）按鈕；鈕扣
(1)	可指實體或螢幕中虛擬的「按鈕」。常搭配動詞push或press [prɛs]（按壓）。

11.	whether [ˋ(h)wɛðɚ]（連接詞）是否

壹、認識間接問句
基本定義：一個完整句子中包含的問句，常為了要簡化數個句子或修飾語氣。
基本句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞…
★最後的標點符號以主要子句為主
例：We are not sure about that. How can they work out the problem?
→ We are not sure how they can work out the problem.
（合併簡化兩個句子，用句號）
Would it be possible for you to tell me about that? Where does he live? 
→ Would it be possible for you to tell me where he lives? （修飾語氣，用問號）
貳、間接問句的句型
一、動詞為be動詞
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + be動詞
例：Please tell me what her name is. （請告訴我她的名字是什麼。）
Can you tell me where the restroom is? （你可以告訴我廁所在哪裡嗎？）
 練習 
 1.	How old is Mrs. Collins?
→ Tell me how old Mrs. Collins is.                                       	
 2.	Why are they not happy on weekends?
→ Do you know why they are not happy on weekends?         	
 3.	What was my sister doing in the kitchen at that time?
→ Grandpa asked me what my sister was doing in the kitchen at that time.        
 4.	你知道現在幾點嗎？
Do you know what time it is?	
 5.	Ronald不確定他的錢包是在哪裡被偷的。
Ronald is not sure where his wallet was stolen.	
二、動詞為一般動詞（do/does/did）
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞（依時態變化）
例：Let me know what you don’t sell in this store. （讓我知道店裡你不賣什麼。）
She never knows how long it takes her son to go home by bike.
（她從來不知道她兒子騎腳踏車回家需要多久時間。）
They still didn’t know what time Carl woke up this morning.
（他們還是不知道Carl今天早上幾點醒來。）
 練習 
1.	What did they talk about to each other?
→ I couldn’t hear what they talked about to each other.                         
2.	How long does he have to stay?
→Do you have any idea how long he has to stay?                           	
3.	Who do you not invite?
→ Is there any chance that you could tell me who you do not invite?             
4.	我想知道你以前在哪所高中上學。（I’d…）
I’d like to know which senior high school you went to before.	 
5.	我需要知道她為什麼常常晚回家。
I need to know why she often comes home late.	 
三、動詞為情態助動詞（can/could/may/might/will/would/should/must）
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + 情態助動詞 + 原形動詞
例：The teacher taught those kids what they mustn’t do.
（這位老師教那群小孩他們不准做什麼。）
Please let me know where my shoes can be placed. 
（請讓我知道鞋子可以放在哪裡。）
 練習 
1.	Whose towel can this be?
→ No one tells me whose towel this can be.	
2.	Who would you borrow the money from?

四、時態為現在完成式
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + have/has p.p.
例：I don’t really understand why Rebecca has always loved shopping. 
（我真的不瞭解為什麼Rebecca一直以來都很喜歡逛街購物。）
Mr. Cooper can’t remember how long he has known his wife. 
（Cooper先生記不得跟他老婆認識多久了。）
 練習 
1.	When have the police caught the bad guy?
→ Everyone here has been told when the police have caught the bad guy.         
2.	Which country has your aunt gone to?
→ Make sure which country your aunt has gone to.                             
3.	Since when have you been sick?
→ Does your husband know since when you have been sick?                    
4.	猜猜看我在這邊多久了。
Guess how long I have been here.	 
5.	有人知道為什麼Jennifer還沒寫完功課嗎？

五、疑問詞為主詞
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + 動詞
例：Guess what is on her mind.（猜猜看她在想什麼。）
Does Linda know who left me the note?（Linda知道誰留紙條給我的嗎？）
 練習 
1.	Who started the fight?
→ Be honest and tell me who started the fight.

→ Be sure to ask Gloria who turned up the radio and made such loud noises.      
3.	What was covered by the book on the top shelf?

六、名詞片語
句型：主要子句 + 疑問詞 + to + 原形動詞
例：The students are thinking about how to make a kite. 
（學生們正在思考要怎麼做風箏。）
Mrs. Moore isn’t sure when to take the medicine.
（Moore太太不確定什麼時候要吃藥。）
★	間接問句的主詞需與主要子句的主詞或受詞一致時，才能省略後形成片語
★	疑問詞why不適於此用法
 練習  用名詞片語合併
1.	He wants to know that. Which book should he take?
→ He wants to know which book to take.                                	  	 
2.	Jeff couldn’t remember that. Who could he ask for help?
→ Jeff couldn’t remember who to ask for help.                            	  	 
3.	Can you tell me about that? When can I leave?
→ Can you tell me when to leave?                                   	     
4.	他知道該去何處嗎？
Does he know where to go?												 
5.	Brian的媽媽不曉得如何跟你道歉。 
Brian’s mother doesn’t know how to say sorry to you.							 
七、whether/if（是否）所引導的間接問句
句型：主要子句 + whether/if + 主詞 + 動詞…（or not）.
例：She forgot whether/if she fed the cat (or not).（她忘了是否有餵過貓。）
The fisherman wants to know whether/if the wind is blowing from the east (or not). （這位漁夫想知道風是否從東邊吹過來。）
★有名詞片語whether to…的用法，但不適用於if to…
 練習 
1.	Has the postman been here?
→ Ask him whether/if the postman has been here (or not).                      
2.	Do they have to go up the stairs?
→ Does Carol have any idea whether/if they have to go up the stairs (or not)?     
3.	Would you like my gift?
→ I wasn’t sure whether/if you would like my gift (or not).                        
4.	我不在乎是否會下雨。
I don’t care whether/if it will rain (or not).                                      
5.	Margaret問她男朋友是否計畫要去游泳。
Margaret asked her boyfriend whether/if he planned to swim (or not).	             
參、延伸補充
一、when不同用法的比較
當疑問詞，表「何時」：
句中是表示「未來」的時間，時態是「未來式」
例1：Tell me when he will call you.
例2：Do you know when he will come to the office?
當連接詞，表「當…的時候」：
句中是表示「未來」的時間，時態要寫成「現在式」
例1：Talk to him when he calls you tonight.
例2：Will you be happy when he comes to the office?
二、if不同用法的比較
表「是否」：
句中是表示「未來」的時間，時態是「未來式」
例1：Tell me if he will come tomorrow.
例2：	They are not sure if the weather will be nice this Tuesday.
表「如果」：
句中是表示「未來」的時間，時態要寫成「現在式」
例1：	Tell him the truth if he comes tomorrow.
例2：	Go for a picnic if the weather is nice this Tuesday.
