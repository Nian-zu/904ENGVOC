## 鈴木一朗的 262 支安打
On October 1, 2004, Ichiro Suzuki of the Seattle Mariners got his 258th hit of the season. With that hit, he broke a record that was 84 years old. In 1920, George Sisler had 257 hits in one season, and nobody had done better since then. Ichiro finished the season with 262 hits. My grandpa was a big fan of Ichiro. He still keeps an old newspaper from that day in a drawer. Last weekend, he showed it to me. The [[paper|soup|tea|juice]] was yellow, and the corners were broken. Under the big photo of Ichiro, Grandpa had drawn a line with a [[ruler|spoon|pillow|towel]]. Next to the line, he had written "262!" with a red [[pencil|blanket|fork|bowl]]. "Be careful," Grandpa said. "Don't use [[scissors|blankets|pillows|towels]] near it. I don't want to lose even a small piece." I promised to [[protect|fry|boil|eat]] it for him.
> 1 這裡的 paper 指前一句的 newspaper（報紙），舊報紙會變黃、角落破損。soup（湯）、tea（茶）、juice（果汁）都是液體，不會有「角落」，也不是收在抽屜裡的東西。
> 2 用 ruler（尺）畫一條線。spoon（湯匙）、pillow（枕頭）、towel（毛巾）都畫不出直線。
> 3 用紅色 pencil（鉛筆）寫字。blanket、fork、bowl 都不是寫字的工具。
> 4 不要在報紙旁邊用 scissors（剪刀），免得剪壞。blankets、pillows、towels 不會剪掉報紙的一小塊。
> 5 protect「保護」：答應幫爺爺保護這份報紙。fry（油炸）、boil（煮沸）、eat（吃）都不合理。
+ Ichiro Suzuki = 鈴木一朗（日本棒球選手）
+ Seattle Mariners = 西雅圖水手隊
+ George Sisler = 喬治・席斯勒（美國早期棒球選手）
+ hit = （棒球的）安打
+ corner = 角落

= 我們的班導王老師星期五要過四十歲生日，全班想給她一個驚喜。星期四放學後，我們留在教室裡。Amy 用尺在黑板上畫了一個大大的直框，大家用彩色粉筆在框裡寫下短短的祝福。有些同學不喜歡在黑板上寫字，就把祝福寫在小紙片上，再用膠帶貼上去。Leo 用剪刀剪了四十顆小愛心，一歲一顆。隔天早上，王老師一打開門就愣住了。她看著黑板看了很久，然後說：「這是我收過最棒的生日卡。」上課前，她把黑板拍了下來。那個星期剩下的幾天，沒有人想擦黑板。

## 一千隻紙鶴
My classmate Kelly broke her leg in a car accident and had to stay in the hospital for a month. Our class wanted to do something for her. "In Japan, people fold one thousand paper cranes for a sick friend," said our teacher. "They hope the friend can get well soon." So we decided to try. Every day, we spent lunch break folding cranes. First, we needed square paper. We used a [[ruler|towel|pillow|spoon]] to draw squares on big sheets of colorful [[paper|soup|juice|milk]]. Then we cut them out with [[scissors|pencils|erasers|blankets]]. Some cranes were not pretty, but nobody cared. Our class leader wrote the number of cranes on the [[blackboard|pillow|sandwich|plate]] every afternoon: 120, 380, 650… After three weeks, we finally reached one thousand! We put them on long strings and [[brought|boiled|fried|washed]] them to the hospital. When Kelly saw the cranes, she cried and smiled at the same time.
> 1 用 ruler（尺）畫正方形。towel、pillow、spoon 都畫不出直線。
> 2 sheets of colorful paper「彩色的紙」。soup、juice、milk 是液體，不能畫正方形。
> 3 用 scissors（剪刀）剪下來。pencils、erasers、blankets 都不能剪紙。
> 4 班長每天把數量寫在 blackboard（黑板）上讓大家看。pillow、sandwich、plate 都不是寫字給全班看的地方。
> 5 bring … to the hospital「把…帶到醫院」（過去式 brought）。boil、fry、wash 都和送紙鶴到醫院無關。
+ crane = 鶴
+ square = 正方形（的）
+ string = 線、細繩
+ get well = 康復
= 我的同學 Kelly 在一場車禍中摔斷了腿，必須住院一個月。我們班想為她做點什麼。「在日本，大家會為生病的朋友摺一千隻紙鶴，」老師說，「希望朋友能早日康復。」於是我們決定試試看。每天午休時間，我們都在摺紙鶴。首先要有正方形的紙，我們用尺在一大張一大張的彩色紙上畫出正方形，再用剪刀剪下來。有些紙鶴摺得不漂亮，但沒有人在意。班長每天下午都把紙鶴的數量寫在黑板上：120、380、650……三個星期後，我們終於摺滿一千隻了！我們用長線把紙鶴串起來，帶到醫院去。Kelly 看到紙鶴的時候，又哭又笑。

## 阿嬤的窗花
Every Chinese New Year, my grandma makes paper cuttings for our windows. This year, I asked her to teach me. She gave me a square piece of red [[paper|soup|juice|tea]] and told me to fold it into a small triangle. Then she drew half of a flower on it with a [[pencil|spoon|towel|bowl]]. "Now cut along the lines," she said, and gave me a small pair of [[scissors|pillows|blankets|candles]]. I was very careful, but I still cut too much on one side. When I opened the paper, the flower had a big hole in the middle. "It looks like a donut," I said. Grandma laughed. "That is fine. Every artist makes mistakes." My second try was much better. My little brother wanted to help too, but he was only five. So Grandma gave him a [[ruler|knife|soup|juice]] and asked him to measure the windows for us. On New Year's Eve, our windows were full of red flowers, and Grandma said I was a real [[artist|dentist|nurse|clerk]] now.
> 1 a square piece of red paper「一張正方形的紅紙」。soup、juice、tea 是液體，不能摺成三角形。
> 2 用 pencil（鉛筆）在紙上畫出半朵花。spoon、towel、bowl 都不能畫線。
> 3 a small pair of scissors「一把小剪刀」，用來沿線剪。pillows、blankets、candles 都不能剪紙。
> 4 給五歲弟弟一把 ruler（尺）量窗戶，安全又能量長度。knife（刀子）不能量東西也不適合給小孩；soup、juice 是液體。
> 5 剪紙是藝術，所以說我是真正的 artist（藝術家）。dentist（牙醫）、nurse（護士）、clerk（店員）都和剪紙無關。
+ paper cutting = 剪紙
+ triangle = 三角形
+ donut = 甜甜圈
+ New Year's Eve = 除夕
+ artist = 藝術家
= 每年過年，我阿嬤都會剪窗花貼在我們家的窗戶上。今年我請她教我。她給我一張正方形的紅紙，要我把它摺成一個小三角形。接著她用鉛筆在上面畫了半朵花。「現在沿著線剪。」她說，然後給我一把小剪刀。我很小心，但還是有一邊剪太多了。打開紙時，花的中間有一個大洞。「看起來像甜甜圈。」我說。阿嬤笑了：「沒關係，每個藝術家都會犯錯。」我第二次剪得好多了。我五歲的弟弟也想幫忙，但他太小了，所以阿嬤給他一把尺，請他幫我們量窗戶。除夕夜，我們家的窗戶貼滿了紅色的花，阿嬤說我現在是一個真正的藝術家了。

## 忘了帶鉛筆的考試
On the morning of the big math exam, I opened my pencil case and found nothing inside. I forgot to put my [[pencils|spoons|socks|towels]] back after doing homework last night! The exam was going to start in five minutes. I was so [[nervous|thirsty|hungry|sleepy]] that my hands started to shake. Then my classmate Eric turned around. He did not say a word. He just put two pencils and an eraser on my desk. "I always bring extra," he said with a smile. Our teacher handed out the test [[paper|soup|juice|milk]]. There was a question about a triangle, so I needed to draw straight lines. Eric passed me his [[ruler|blanket|pillow|candle]] too. I finished the exam on time. After the test, the teacher wrote the answers on the [[blackboard|sandwich|sofa|sock]], and I found that I got most of them right. Since that day, I always check my pencil case before I go to bed. And I always bring extra pencils, just like Eric.
> 1 筆袋裡空空的，是忘了把 pencils（鉛筆）放回去。spoons、socks、towels 本來就不會放在筆袋裡。
> 2 so nervous that …「緊張到…」，手開始發抖。thirsty、hungry、sleepy 和「考試前發現沒帶筆」的情緒不合，也不會讓手發抖。
> 3 test paper「考卷」。soup、juice、milk 是液體，老師不會發這些當考試用。
> 4 畫三角形的直線要用 ruler（尺）。blanket、pillow、candle 都畫不出直線。
> 5 老師把答案寫在 blackboard（黑板）上。sandwich、sofa、sock 都不是寫字給全班看的地方。
+ pencil case = 筆袋
+ shake = 發抖
+ extra = 額外的
+ hand out = 發下
+ triangle = 三角形
= 數學大考那天早上，我打開筆袋，發現裡面什麼都沒有。我昨晚寫完功課忘了把鉛筆放回去！考試再五分鐘就要開始了，我緊張到手開始發抖。這時候，我的同學 Eric 轉過頭來，一句話也沒說，只是把兩枝鉛筆和一塊橡皮擦放在我桌上。「我都會多帶一些。」他笑著說。老師發下考卷，有一題跟三角形有關，我需要畫直線，Eric 又把他的尺借給我。我準時寫完了考試。考完後，老師把答案寫在黑板上，我發現大部分都答對了。從那天起，我每天睡前都會檢查筆袋，而且我也像 Eric 一樣，總是多帶幾枝鉛筆。

## 畫什麼就變什麼的鉛筆
One night, a strange old woman gave Lily a pencil. "Whatever you draw with this [[pencil|spoon|towel|bowl]] will become real," she said, and then she was gone. Lily did not believe her, but she tried it anyway. She drew a small cat on a piece of [[paper|soup|juice|tea]]. Suddenly, a real cat jumped out and sat on her bed! Lily was so excited. The next morning, she drew an umbrella because it was raining, and it worked again. At school, Lily wanted to show her friends. She drew a big birthday cake on the [[blackboard|soup|juice|pillow]]. But the cake became so big that it filled half of the classroom! Everyone laughed, but the teacher was not happy. Lily quickly took out her [[scissors|soup|juice|milk]] and tried to cut the cake into small pieces. Then she used a [[ruler|towel|pillow|blanket]] to make sure every piece was the same size. The whole class shared the cake. From then on, Lily only drew small things.
> 1 with this pencil「用這枝鉛筆畫」，前一句老婆婆給的就是鉛筆。spoon、towel、bowl 都不能畫圖。
> 2 a piece of paper 一張紙，在上面畫貓。soup、juice、tea 是液體。
> 3 在教室的 blackboard（黑板）上畫一個大蛋糕。soup、juice 是液體，pillow 不會在教室裡讓人畫大圖。
> 4 用 scissors（剪刀）把蛋糕剪成小塊（故事裡的誇張情節）。soup、juice、milk 是液體，不能切東西。
> 5 用 ruler（尺）量，確定每塊一樣大。towel、pillow、blanket 都不能量大小。
+ whatever = 無論什麼
+ real = 真的
+ suddenly = 突然
+ make sure = 確定
= 有一天晚上，一位奇怪的老婆婆給了 Lily 一枝鉛筆。「你用這枝鉛筆畫什麼，什麼就會變成真的。」她說完就不見了。Lily 不相信，但還是試了一下。她在一張紙上畫了一隻小貓，突然間，一隻真的貓跳出來，坐在她的床上！Lily 興奮極了。隔天早上下雨，她畫了一把雨傘，又成功了。到了學校，Lily 想讓朋友看看。她在黑板上畫了一個大大的生日蛋糕，結果蛋糕變得好大，塞滿了半間教室！大家都笑了，但老師一點也不開心。Lily 趕快拿出剪刀，想把蛋糕剪成小塊，再用尺確定每塊都一樣大。全班一起分享了蛋糕。從此以後，Lily 只畫小東西。
