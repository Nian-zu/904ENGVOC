We were excited about our PE class, but it suddenly started to rain heavily. "Sorry, everyone. We can't play [[outside|ago|else|seldom]] today," said Ms. Wang. We all looked sad because we wanted to run on the field. Instead, she took us to the gym and gave us a [[choice|habit|factory|land]]: basketball or dodgeball. Most boys chose basketball, and most girls chose dodgeball. Soon the gym was [[full|lucky|proud|modern]] of noise and laughter. Even the quiet students were running and shouting. Ms. Wang joined the girls' game and threw the ball very hard. After class, we were all tired and thirsty, so Ms. Wang [[treated|fed|hunted|landed]] us to cold drinks from the shop next to the gym. Everyone cheered for her. It was a rainy day, but everyone had a lot of [[fun|space|death|health]].
> 1 play outside「在戶外玩」：下雨不能在外面上課。ago 要接在時間後面；else 要接在 something、anyone 等字後面；seldom（很少）不能放在句尾修飾 play。
> 2 give + 人 + a choice「讓某人選擇」：籃球或躲避球二選一。habit（習慣）、factory（工廠）、land（土地）後面都不能接「籃球或躲避球」。
> 3 be full of noise「充滿吵鬧聲」。lucky、proud、modern 都不能接 of noise 形容體育館。
> 4 treat + 人 + to + 物「請某人吃／喝…」：老師請大家喝冷飲。fed us to、hunted us to、landed us to 都不是正確用法。
> 5 have fun「玩得開心」：a lot of fun 非常開心。have a lot of space（空間）、death（死亡）、health（健康）都不合語意。
+ PE = 體育課
+ gym = 體育館
+ dodgeball = 躲避球
+ laughter = 笑聲
= 我們本來很期待體育課，但突然下起了大雨。「抱歉，各位，今天不能在外面上課了。」王老師說。大家都很失望，因為我們想在操場上跑步。結果，她帶我們去體育館，讓我們自己選：打籃球或打躲避球。大部分男生選籃球，大部分女生選躲避球。很快地，體育館裡就充滿了吵鬧聲和笑聲，連平常安靜的同學都在跑來跑去、大聲喊叫。王老師還加入女生的比賽，把球丟得超用力。下課後，我們都又累又渴，所以王老師請大家去體育館旁的商店喝冷飲。大家都為她歡呼。雖然是個下雨天，但每個人都玩得很開心。
