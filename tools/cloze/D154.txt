## 公園裡的尋寶遊戲
For my brother's tenth birthday, Dad planned a treasure [[hunt|habit|factory|land]] in the park. He hid ten small notes around the park, and each note told us where to find the next one. The first note said, "Go to the place where people [[feed|hunt|clear|land]] the ducks." We ran to the pond and found the second note [[beside|ago|either|else]] a big rock. The next notes took us to the slide, the old bridge, and a flower garden. My brother read every note out loud and ran ahead of me. The last note said, "The treasure is in the tallest tree." In the tree, we found a box [[full|clear|proud|lucky]] of toy cars and chocolate. My brother shouted with joy. We were tired but happy. Dad smiled and said, "Next year, it's your [[turn|habit|factory|land]] to plan the game for me."
> 1 treasure hunt「尋寶遊戲」，hunt 當名詞是「搜尋」。treasure habit、treasure factory、treasure land 都不是遊戲名稱。
> 2 feed the ducks「餵鴨子」：公園池塘邊大家餵鴨子的地方。在公園裡不會 hunt（獵殺）鴨子；clear、land 接 the ducks 也說不通。
> 3 beside「在…旁邊」：在大石頭旁找到第二張紙條。ago 要接在時間後面；either 用在否定句句尾；else 要接在 something 等字後面，都不能當介系詞。
> 4 a box full of …「裝滿…的盒子」。clear of 是「沒有…」，和找到寶物不合；proud、lucky 不能形容盒子裝了東西。
> 5 It's one's turn to …「輪到某人做…」：明年輪到你們規劃。habit、factory、land 都不能用在這個句型。

+ pond = 池塘

= 我弟弟十歲生日時，爸爸在公園裡安排了一場尋寶遊戲。他在公園各處藏了十張小紙條，每張紙條都會告訴我們下一張在哪裡。第一張紙條寫著：「去大家餵鴨子的地方。」我們跑到池塘邊，在一塊大石頭旁邊找到了第二張紙條。接下來的紙條帶我們到溜滑梯、舊木橋和一座花園。弟弟把每張紙條都大聲念出來，跑在我前面。最後一張紙條寫著：「寶藏在最高的那棵樹上。」我們在樹上找到一個裝滿玩具車和巧克力的盒子。弟弟開心地大叫。我們雖然很累，但很開心。爸爸笑著說：「明年換你們來幫我規劃遊戲囉。」

## 選擇困難的弟弟
Last Sunday, Mom [[treated|fed|hunted|landed]] my little brother Leo and me to ice cream. But Leo can never make a [[choice|habit|land|factory]] quickly. At the shop, there were twenty flavors, and he stood in front of the window for fifteen minutes. Chocolate or strawberry? Mango or milk tea? The line behind us became longer and longer. The woman at the counter let him taste three flavors for free, but he still could not [[decide|own|collect|celebrate]] which one to buy. People behind us started to look angry. Finally, I said, "Let's buy chocolate and mango and [[share|feed|hunt|land]] them." Leo smiled and agreed at once. The ice cream was delicious. Now, every time we go to that shop, we buy two flavors. Sharing has become our happy little [[habit|factory|land|health]].
> 1 treat + 人 + to + 物「請某人吃…」：媽媽請我們吃冰淇淋。fed us to、hunted us to、landed us to 都不是正確用法。
> 2 make a choice「做選擇」：Leo 永遠沒辦法很快做決定。make a habit 要加 of 才是「養成…習慣」；make a land、make a factory 都說不通。
> 3 decide which…「決定要哪一個」。own（擁有）、collect（收集）、celebrate（慶祝）都不能接 which one to buy。
> 4 share「分享」：買兩種口味一起吃。feed（餵）、hunt（打獵）、land（降落）接 them（冰淇淋）都說不通。
> 5 habit「習慣」：一起分享成了我們的小習慣。factory（工廠）、land（土地）、health（健康）都不能形容「每次都買兩種」這件事。
+ flavor = 口味
+ strawberry = 草莓
+ counter = 櫃檯
= 上星期天，媽媽請我和弟弟 Leo 吃冰淇淋。但 Leo 永遠沒辦法很快做出選擇。店裡有二十種口味，他在玻璃櫃前站了十五分鐘。巧克力還是草莓？芒果還是奶茶？我們後面排隊的人越來越多。櫃檯的阿姨讓他免費試吃三種口味，但他還是決定不了要買哪一種。我們後面的人開始露出不耐煩的表情。最後，我說：「我們買巧克力和芒果，一起分著吃吧。」Leo 笑了，馬上就答應。冰淇淋很好吃。現在，每次我們去那家店，都會買兩種口味。一起分享成了我們快樂的小習慣。
