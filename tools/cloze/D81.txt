## 丹麥郵政送出最後一封信
On December 30, 2025, PostNord, Denmark's national postal company, delivered its last letter. For 401 years, Danish [[mail|weather|fever|traffic]] carriers had brought letters to homes across the country. But today most people send messages on their phones, so the number of letters had dropped sharply. PostNord still delivers packages, and a private company called Dao now handles most letters in Denmark. My grandma read the news and sighed. She still [[mails|boils|bakes|irons]] a birthday card to her sister in Tainan every year. "A card you can hold is different from a message on a screen," she said. My uncle is a [[lawyer|pumpkin|candle|towel]], and his [[boss|lemon|blanket|spoon]] still writes thank-you notes by hand to everyone in the office. So maybe paper letters will not disappear completely. This year, I'm going to [[mail|cough|cry|sleep]] a real card to my best friend instead of just sending her a message.
> 1 mail carriers「郵差、送信的人」，mail 在這裡當名詞，表示「郵件」。weather（天氣）、fever（發燒）、traffic（交通）都不能和 carriers 組成「送信的人」。
> 2 mails a birthday card to her sister「把生日卡寄給她妹妹」，mail 當動詞是「郵寄」，主詞 She 是第三人稱單數所以加 s。boils（煮沸）、bakes（烘烤）、irons（燙衣服）都不能用在卡片上。
> 3 My uncle is a lawyer「我舅舅是律師」，說明舅舅的職業。pumpkin、candle、towel 都不是職業。
> 4 his boss still writes thank-you notes「他的老闆還會手寫感謝卡」，會寫卡片給辦公室每個人的是老闆。lemon、blanket、spoon 都不會寫字。
> 5 mail a real card to my best friend「寄一張真正的卡片給我最好的朋友」，be going to 後面接原形動詞 mail。cough、cry、sleep 都是不及物動詞，後面不能直接接 a real card。
+ postal = 郵政的
+ deliver = 遞送
+ carrier = 遞送者
+ package = 包裹
+ disappear = 消失
= 2025 年 12 月 30 日，丹麥的國家郵政公司 PostNord 送出了最後一封信。四百零一年來，丹麥的郵差把信件送到全國各地的家庭。但現在大多數人都用手機傳訊息，所以信件的數量大幅減少。PostNord 仍然會遞送包裹，而在丹麥，現在大部分的信件改由一家叫 Dao 的民營公司處理。我奶奶看到這則新聞時嘆了一口氣。她每年還是會寄生日卡給住在台南的妹妹。「拿得到的卡片和螢幕上的訊息是不一樣的。」她說。我舅舅是律師，他的老闆到現在還會手寫感謝卡給辦公室裡的每個人。所以也許紙本信件不會完全消失。今年，我要寄一張真正的卡片給我最好的朋友，而不只是傳訊息給她。
