## 最後一次穿制服
Today was our graduation day. Everyone wore their school [[uniforms|activities|pleasures|corners]] for the last time. My mom took many photos of me and my friends in front of the school gate. During the ceremony, our principal said, "It has been a [[pleasure|uniform|corner|garbage]] to watch you all grow up." Then our class leader gave a speech. She said she was [[glad|empty|national|delicious]] to have such wonderful classmates. When she talked about our class trip to Kenting, many people started to cry. After the ceremony, we walked [[along|during|until|since]] the school hallway one last time. On the wall, there were photos of all our school [[activities|uniforms|pleasures|corners]]: the sports day, the singing contest, and the school festival. I found myself in one of the photos, laughing with my friends. I will miss this school very much.
> 1 wear school uniforms「穿制服」。activities（活動）、pleasures（樂趣）、corners（角落）都不能「穿」。
> 2 It has been a pleasure to…「…一直是件愉快的事」。uniform、corner、garbage 放進去都說不通。
> 3 be glad to + 原形動詞「很高興…」。empty、national、delicious 都不能接 to have 表示心情。
> 4 walk along the hallway「沿著走廊走」。during、until、since 都是跟時間有關的介系詞，不能接 the school hallway 表示路線。
> 5 school activities「學校活動」，冒號後面舉運動會、合唱比賽、園遊會。uniforms、pleasures、corners 都不能用這些例子說明。
+ graduation = 畢業
+ ceremony = 典禮
+ principal = 校長
+ speech = 演講；致詞
+ hallway = 走廊
= 今天是我們的畢業典禮，大家最後一次穿上學校制服。媽媽在校門口幫我和朋友們拍了好多照片。典禮上，校長說：「看著你們長大，一直是件很開心的事。」接著班長上台致詞，她說很高興能有這麼棒的同學。說到我們班去墾丁的畢業旅行時，很多人都哭了。典禮結束後，我們最後一次沿著學校的走廊走。牆上貼著我們所有學校活動的照片：運動會、合唱比賽和園遊會。我在其中一張照片裡找到自己，正和朋友們笑成一團。我會非常想念這所學校。

## 勇敢說「住手」
My classmate Tim was always mean to a quiet boy named Leo. He took Leo's things and laughed at his clothes. Everyone knew it was wrong, but nobody said anything. One day, I decided to be [[brave|empty|national|delicious]]. When Tim grabbed Leo's bag, I stood up and said, "Stop it. Give it back." Tim was surprised. He didn't [[expect|reach|spell|seem]] anyone to stop him. Then two more classmates stood up [[along|during|until|since]] with me. Tim looked around, gave the bag back, and walked away. Later, I told our teacher about it. She talked to Tim, and he said sorry to Leo. After school, Leo came to me and said, "I'm so [[glad|empty|national|delicious]] you helped me. Thank you." "It's my [[pleasure|uniform|corner|garbage]]," I said. Now Leo and I are good friends, and we eat lunch together every day.
> 1 decide to be brave「決定鼓起勇氣」。empty、national、delicious 都不能形容人鼓起勇氣。
> 2 expect + 人 + to + 原形動詞「預期某人會…」，他沒想到有人會阻止他。reach、spell 接 anyone to stop him 說不通；seem 不能接受詞。
> 3 along with「和…一起」，另外兩位同學和我一起站起來。during、until、since 都不能和 with 搭配。
> 4 be glad (that)…「很高興…」。empty、national、delicious 都不能接子句表示心情。
> 5 It's my pleasure.「不客氣」，回應道謝。uniform、corner、garbage 放進去都說不通。
+ mean = 刻薄的；壞心的
+ grab = 抓；搶
= 我的同學 Tim 總是欺負一個叫 Leo 的安靜男生。他會拿走 Leo 的東西，還嘲笑他的衣服。大家都知道這樣不對，卻沒有人說話。有一天，我決定鼓起勇氣。Tim 搶走 Leo 的書包時，我站起來說：「住手，還給他。」Tim 很驚訝，他沒想到會有人阻止他。接著又有兩位同學和我一起站了起來。Tim 看了看四周，把書包還回去就走開了。後來我把這件事告訴老師。她找 Tim 談過，Tim 也向 Leo 道歉了。放學後，Leo 走過來對我說：「我好高興你幫了我，謝謝你。」「不客氣。」我說。現在 Leo 和我是好朋友，我們每天都一起吃午餐。
