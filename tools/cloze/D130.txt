## 火車上的老先生
Last Sunday, my mom and I took the train from Taipei to Hualien to visit my aunt. An old man sat next to us. About thirty minutes into the trip, he began to [[cough|borrow|decorate|collect]] again and again. His face looked red, and his hands were shaking. Mom asked, "Sir, are you all right?" He said he had a [[fever|ticket|village|museum]] this morning but did not want to miss his granddaughter's birthday party. He also forgot to bring his [[medicine|garbage|weather|traffic]]. Mom always carries a small first-aid bag, so she gave him some water and a pill for fever. She also [[called|borrowed|collected|decorated]] the train staff, and they brought him a blanket. When we arrived in Hualien, the old man looked much better. He thanked us again and again. "I hope you stay [[healthy|crowded|expensive|national]] and happy," he said with a smile. Then he walked out to meet his family.
> 1 began to cough 開始咳嗽。borrow、decorate、collect 都要接受詞，而且和臉紅、手發抖的病況無關。
> 2 had a fever 早上發燒。ticket、village、museum 都不是病況。
> 3 forgot to bring his medicine 忘了帶藥。garbage、weather、traffic 都不是生病的人該帶的東西。

## 我想當小兒科醫生
When I was six, I had a high [[fever|ticket|corner|bill]] and stayed in the hospital for a week. I was scared of the needles and the white walls. But one doctor, Dr. Lee, always made me laugh. She wore funny socks and told me stories about a brave little bear who always took his [[medicine|weather|traffic|garbage]] without crying. Because of her, I was not afraid to take my pills. Since then, I have had a [[dream|bill|corner|ticket]]: I want to be a doctor for kids like her. Now I am in ninth grade, and I study science very hard. I also volunteer at a clinic on weekends. I help nurses give out masks to children who are [[coughing|borrowing|inviting|collecting]], and I read picture books to kids who are waiting. Last week, a little girl with the [[flu|uniform|furniture|museum]] held my hand and said, "You will be a good doctor." Her words made me want to work even harder.
> 1 a high fever 高燒，住院一週。ticket、corner、bill 都不是病況。
> 2 take medicine 吃藥：故事裡的小熊吃藥都不哭。weather（天氣）、traffic（交通）、garbage（垃圾）都不能「吃」。
> 3 have a dream「有一個夢想」（D169）：想當小兒科醫生。bill（帳單）、corner（角落）、ticket（票）都不能接冒號說出「想當醫生」。
> 4 children who are coughing 正在咳嗽的孩子，所以發口罩。borrowing、inviting、collecting 都要接受詞，也不是要戴口罩的理由。
> 5 a girl with the flu 得了流感的女孩。uniform、furniture、museum 都不是讓她來診所的原因。

+ clinic = 診所

= 我六歲時發高燒，在醫院住了一個星期。我很怕打針，也怕白色的牆。但有一位李醫生總是逗我笑。她穿著有趣的襪子，跟我講一隻勇敢小熊的故事，那隻小熊吃藥從來不哭。因為她，我不再害怕吃藥丸。從那時候起，我就有一個夢想：我想像她一樣，當一個照顧小朋友的醫生。現在我九年級了，我非常用功地讀自然科學。我週末也在一間診所當志工，幫護理師發口罩給在咳嗽的小朋友，還讀繪本給在候診的孩子聽。上週，一個得了流感的小女孩握著我的手說：「你會是一個好醫生。」她的話讓我想更加努力。

## 打流感疫苗的早上
Every October, nurses come to our school to give students flu shots. This year, my best friend Tina was very nervous. "I hate needles," she said. "Why do I need a shot? I am [[healthy|crowded|noisy|expensive]]!" The nurse explained, "The shot helps your body fight the [[flu|ticket|garbage|village]] before you get sick. It is better than having a high fever and taking [[medicine|weather|history|traffic]] for a week." Tina still looked scared, so I held her hand. "Look at the window and count to ten," I said. Before she reached five, it was over. "That's it?" she asked in surprise. In December, the flu went around our school. Many students had a [[fever|bill|corner|sentence]] or a bad [[cough|pocket|lesson|festival]], but Tina and I were fine. "Okay," Tina said. "I will not be afraid of that needle next year."
> 1 I am healthy!「我很健康！」：她覺得自己沒生病，不需要打針。crowded、noisy、expensive 都不能當作不用打針的理由。
> 2 fight the flu 對抗流感。ticket、garbage、village 都不是身體要對抗的病。
> 3 take medicine for a week 吃一週的藥。weather、history、traffic 都不能吃。
> 4 had a fever 發燒。bill、corner、sentence 都不是病況。
> 5 a bad cough 咳得很厲害。pocket、lesson、festival 都不是病況。
+ nurse = 護理師
+ flu shot = 流感疫苗注射
+ needle = 針
= 每年十月，護理師都會來我們學校替學生打流感疫苗。今年，我最好的朋友蒂娜非常緊張。「我討厭打針，」她說，「我為什麼要打針？我很健康啊！」護理師解釋：「疫苗能在你生病之前，幫助你的身體對抗流感。這比發高燒、吃一個禮拜的藥好多了。」蒂娜看起來還是很害怕，所以我握住她的手。「看著窗戶，數到十。」我說。她還沒數到五，就打完了。「就這樣？」她驚訝地問。十二月時，流感在我們學校流行起來。很多學生發燒或咳得很厲害，但蒂娜和我都沒事。「好吧，」蒂娜說，「明年我不會再怕那支針了。」
