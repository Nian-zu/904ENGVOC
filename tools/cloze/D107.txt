She pressed it, and the door opened by itself. Inside was a [[forest|cough|lesson|fever]] full of glowing trees. A small fox in a red hat walked up to her. "Welcome!" it said. "We've been waiting for you. Our forest is dark because the Moon Candle went out. Only a human can light it again." The fox gave her a silver [[key|tiger|fever|pencil]] and led her to a tall tower. Lily opened the door with the key and climbed the stairs. At the top, there was a huge [[candle|homework|rainbow|cough]]. She lit it with a match, and the whole forest became bright. The animals cheered. The fox gave her a tiny glass [[bottle|lesson|monkey|fever]] full of moonlight as a thank-you gift. When Lily walked out, she was back on the street, and the blue door was gone.
> 1 a golden doorbell next to the door「門旁邊有一個金色門鈴」，按下去門就開了。river、cough、lesson 都不能按。
> 2 a forest full of glowing trees「滿是發光樹木的森林」。cough、lesson、fever 都不能「充滿樹木」。
> 3 a silver key「一把銀鑰匙」，用來打開塔的門。tiger、fever、pencil 都不能開門。
> 4 a huge candle「一根巨大的蠟燭」，就是熄滅的月亮蠟燭，用火柴點燃。homework、rainbow、cough 都不能點燃。
> 5 a tiny glass bottle full of moonlight「裝滿月光的小玻璃瓶」。lesson、monkey、fever 都不是玻璃做的容器。
+ glowing = 發光的
+ fox = 狐狸
+ human = 人類
+ tower = 塔
+ match = 火柴
= 放學回家的路上，Lily 注意到兩間店之間有一扇奇怪的藍色門，她以前從沒看過。門旁邊有一個金色門鈴，她按下去，門就自己打開了。裡面是一座滿是發光樹木的森林。一隻戴紅帽子的小狐狸走向她：「歡迎！我們一直在等你。月亮蠟燭熄了，所以我們的森林變暗了，只有人類能再把它點亮。」狐狸給她一把銀鑰匙，帶她到一座高塔前。Lily 用鑰匙打開門，爬上樓梯，塔頂有一根巨大的蠟燭。她用火柴點燃它，整座森林都亮了起來，動物們歡呼著。狐狸送她一個裝滿月光的小玻璃瓶當謝禮。Lily 走出來時，又回到了街上，而那扇藍色的門已經不見了。

## 小狗叼走了鑰匙
Every morning, Dad puts his car [[keys|rivers|lessons|fevers]] on the small table by the door. But last Monday, they were gone. "I'm going to be late for work!" he shouted. We all started looking. Mom looked in the kitchen. My sister looked under the sofa. I looked in the bathroom and even checked the [[towels|tigers|coughs|rainbows]] on the shelf. Nothing. Then I noticed our puppy, Bobo, lying in his bed. He looked very happy and very [[quiet|salty|sour|sweet]]. Too quiet. I moved his blanket and found the keys, a sock, and an empty water [[bottle|pencil|rainbow|fever]]! Bobo had made his own treasure box. Dad laughed, even though he was late. That evening, he bought a small box with a lock for his keys. He also bought Bobo a toy shaped like a [[key|monkey|cough|lesson]]. Now Bobo carries his own key around the house all day.
> 1 his car keys「他的車鑰匙」，不見了會讓他上班遲到。rivers、lessons、fevers 都不能放在桌上、跟上班有關。
> 2 check the towels on the shelf「檢查架子上的毛巾」。tigers、coughs、rainbows 都不會放在浴室的架子上。
> 3 very quiet. Too quiet.「很安靜，安靜得太過頭了」，後面重複 quiet。salty、sour、sweet 都是味道，不能形容狗的樣子。
> 4 an empty water bottle「一個空水瓶」。pencil、rainbow、fever 都不能和 water 組成「水的容器」。
> 5 a toy shaped like a key「鑰匙形狀的玩具」，後面說 Bobo 帶著自己的鑰匙。monkey、cough、lesson 都和後面 his own key 不合。
+ puppy = 小狗
+ treasure box = 寶物箱
+ lock = 鎖
+ shaped like = 形狀像…
= 每天早上，爸爸都把車鑰匙放在門邊的小桌子上。但上星期一，鑰匙不見了。「我上班要遲到了！」他大喊。我們全都開始找：媽媽找廚房，姊姊找沙發底下，我找浴室，連架子上的毛巾都翻了，什麼都沒有。接著我注意到我們的小狗 Bobo 躺在牠的床上，看起來很開心、很安靜——安靜得太過頭了。我掀開牠的毯子，發現了鑰匙、一隻襪子，還有一個空水瓶！Bobo 做了自己的寶物箱。雖然遲到了，爸爸還是笑了。那天晚上，他買了一個附鎖的小盒子放鑰匙，也買給 Bobo 一個鑰匙形狀的玩具。現在 Bobo 整天都叼著自己的「鑰匙」在家裡走來走去。

## 驚喜派對差點穿幫
Next Friday is our teacher Ms. Chen's birthday, so our class planned a surprise party. Everyone had a job. Tom bought the cake, Amy brought thirty [[candles|tigers|rivers|lessons]], and I brought ten [[bottles|coughs|fevers|monkeys]] of soda. We hid everything in the classroom closet. The class leader had the [[key|rainbow|pencil|homework]] to the closet, so it was safe. But on Thursday, Ms. Chen opened the closet to get a map. "Why is there a cake in here?" she asked. Everyone froze. Then Tom said, "It's for... science class! We're studying how cakes change in the heat." Ms. Chen looked at him, smiled, and closed the door. On Friday, we turned off the lights and waited. When she walked in, we shouted, "Surprise!" She laughed and said, "I knew it! But thank you for the science [[lesson|tiger|cough|river]]." Then she wiped her happy tears with a [[towel|fever|monkey|rainbow]] from her bag.
> 1 thirty candles「三十根蠟燭」插在生日蛋糕上。tigers、rivers、lessons 都不能帶來派對插在蛋糕上。
> 2 ten bottles of soda「十瓶汽水」。coughs、fevers、monkeys 都不能裝汽水。
> 3 the key to the closet「櫃子的鑰匙」，所以東西很安全。rainbow、pencil、homework 都不能鎖住櫃子。
> 4 the science lesson「自然課」，老師用 Tom 的藉口開玩笑。tiger、cough、river 都不能和 science 組成「課」。
> 5 wipe her tears with a towel「用毛巾擦眼淚」。fever、monkey、rainbow 都不能擦眼淚。
+ surprise party = 驚喜派對
+ soda = 汽水
+ closet = 櫃子
+ freeze = 僵住（過去式 froze）
+ tear = 眼淚
= 下星期五是陳老師的生日，所以我們班計畫了一個驚喜派對。每個人都有工作：Tom 買蛋糕，Amy 帶三十根蠟燭，我帶十瓶汽水。我們把所有東西藏在教室的櫃子裡，班長有櫃子的鑰匙，所以很安全。但星期四，陳老師打開櫃子要拿地圖。「這裡面為什麼有蛋糕？」她問。大家都僵住了。接著 Tom 說：「那是……自然課用的！我們在研究蛋糕遇熱會怎麼變化。」陳老師看看他，笑了笑就把門關上。星期五，我們關燈等著，她一走進來，我們就大喊：「驚喜！」她笑著說：「我就知道！不過謝謝你們的自然課。」接著她用包包裡的毛巾擦掉開心的眼淚。
