Our guide [[wore|fixed|married|missed]] old-style clothes and told us funny stories about the past. My best friend Jay is [[crazy|able|own|least]] about history, so he asked the guide about a hundred questions. In the garden, there are nine stone turtles with big stone signs on their backs. Visitors are not allowed to climb on them, but we were [[able|own|least|asleep]] to touch their heads. The guide said that touching them brings good luck. Jay touched all nine. "Now I'll get a perfect score on my history test," he said. We'll see!
> 1 have a long history「有悠久的歷史」。hospital、weather、prize 和 long 搭配意思不通。
> 2 fix + 物「修復」：建築被地震和戰爭破壞，人們一再修復它。wore、married、missed it 意思不通。
> 3 wear clothes「穿衣服」：導覽員穿著古裝。fixed、married、missed old-style clothes 意思不通。
> 4 be crazy about + 事物「非常熱愛…」：Jay 超愛歷史。able、own、least about history 意思不通。
> 5 be able to + 動詞「能夠…」：不能爬上去，但可以摸牠們的頭。own、least 不能接 to touch；asleep to touch 意思不通。
