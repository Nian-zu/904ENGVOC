## 最醜的大頭貼
Last Saturday, my best friend Amy and I went to the mall. We found a photo sticker [[machine|season|lesson|letter]] on the third floor. We went inside and put on funny hats. [[While|Than|Whether|However]] the machine was counting down, Amy made a crazy face. I laughed so hard that I closed my eyes. In the next photo, I sneezed. After we finished, the machine printed our stickers. We [[checked|drove|baked|married]] them and laughed again. Every photo was terrible! Then we went to a café [[beside|through|since|until]] the cinema and had some [[delicious|angry|wet|scared]] waffles. Amy put one sticker on her phone case. "These are the best photos ever," she said. I agree. They aren't pretty, but they show how much fun we had together. We plan to take new ones every year.
> 1 photo sticker machine「大頭貼機」。season、lesson、letter 都不能和 photo sticker 組成拍照的機器。
> 2 while「當…的時候」：機器在倒數時，Amy 扮鬼臉。than、whether、however 都不能引導時間子句。
> 3 check「查看」：看看印出來的貼紙。drove、baked、married 都不能接 them（貼紙）表示查看。
> 4 beside「在…旁邊」：電影院旁邊的咖啡廳。through、since、until 都不能表示位置。
> 5 delicious「美味的」：吃了好吃的鬆餅。angry、wet、scared 都不能形容鬆餅。
+ photo sticker = 大頭貼
+ count down = 倒數
+ sneeze = 打噴嚏
+ waffle = 鬆餅
= 上週六，我和最好的朋友 Amy 去逛百貨公司，在三樓發現一台大頭貼機。我們走進去，戴上搞笑的帽子。機器倒數的時候，Amy 扮了一個超誇張的鬼臉，我笑到閉上眼睛；下一張我又打了噴嚏。拍完後，機器印出了我們的貼紙，我們一看又笑了——每一張都慘不忍睹！之後我們去電影院旁邊的咖啡廳吃了好吃的鬆餅。Amy 把一張貼紙貼在手機殼上。「這是史上最棒的照片。」她說。我同意。它們不漂亮，但看得出我們在一起有多開心。我們打算每年都去拍新的。
