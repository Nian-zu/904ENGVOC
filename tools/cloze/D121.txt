## 不想再拿兒童菜單
My brother Leo is twelve, but he looks younger. Every time we go to a restaurant, the waiter gives him the kids' [[menu|noise|traffic|season]] with crayons. Leo hates it. "I am not a little kid anymore," he always says. Last Sunday, we went to a steak house for Dad's birthday. This time, Leo [[ordered|lent|invited|counted]] a big steak, just like Dad. The steak came on a hot iron [[plate|lesson|weather|history]], and it was huge. Leo tried his best, but he could only finish half of it. Then the waiter brought out a small [[bowl|season|distance|museum]] of free ice cream for every child twelve and under. Leo looked at it for a moment. Then he picked up his spoon and smiled. "Well, maybe being young is not so [[terrible|thirsty|wooden|national]]," he said.
> 1 the kids' menu「兒童菜單」，附蠟筆給小朋友。noise、traffic、season 都不能拿給客人點餐。
> 2 order a big steak「點一份大牛排」，ordered 是 order 的過去式。lent（借出）、invited（邀請）、counted（數）都不是點餐的意思。
> 3 hot iron plate「熱鐵板」，牛排放在鐵盤上送來。lesson、weather、history 都不能裝牛排。
> 4 a small bowl of ice cream「一小碗冰淇淋」。season、distance（距離）、museum（博物館）都不能裝冰淇淋。
> 5 not so terrible「沒那麼糟」，拿到免費冰淇淋後改變想法。thirsty（口渴的）、wooden（木製的）、national（國家的）都不能形容「年紀小」這件事。
+ crayon = 蠟筆
+ steak house = 牛排館
+ iron = 鐵製的
= 我弟弟 Leo 十二歲，但他看起來比較小。每次我們去餐廳，服務生都會給他附蠟筆的兒童菜單。Leo 很討厭這樣。「我已經不是小孩子了。」他總是這麼說。上星期天，我們去牛排館幫爸爸慶生。這次 Leo 跟爸爸一樣，點了一份大牛排。牛排放在熱鐵板上送來，份量超大。Leo 盡了全力，卻只吃完一半。接著服務生為每位十二歲以下的小朋友端來一小碗免費的冰淇淋。Leo 看了一會兒，然後拿起湯匙笑了。「嗯，也許年紀小也沒那麼糟。」他說。
