## 用筷子吃義大利麵
Marco is an exchange student from Italy. He lives with my family this year. The first week, he could not use chopsticks at all. When we had [[dumplings|tickets|villages|pockets]] for dinner, they kept falling back onto his plate. He was so embarrassed that his face turned red. So I [[decided|baked|married|borrowed]] to help him. Every night, he practiced picking up beans with chopsticks. After a month, he could eat [[rice|homework|information|knowledge]] from a bowl like a Taiwanese. Then one evening, Marco cooked dinner for us: Italian [[noodles|tickets|offices|seasons]] with tomato sauce. Mom gave everyone a fork, but Marco said, "No, no! Tonight, we use chopsticks!" We all laughed. It was much harder than we thought. My little brother finally gave up and made a spaghetti [[sandwich|ticket|village|museum]] with two pieces of bread. "That's not Italian at all!" Marco shouted, but he ate one too.
