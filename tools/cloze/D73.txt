## 老虎連皮膚都有條紋

Scientists say the pattern is like a person's fingerprint, so they [[use|sleep|arrive|happen]] photos to tell different tigers apart. But why does such a big orange cat have stripes at all? Most animals that tigers hunt, such as deer, cannot see the color orange well. To them, a [[tiger|ladder|sofa|towel]] looks green or brown, just like the plants around it. The stripes break up its shape, so the animal is very hard to [[notice|cry|arrive|laugh]] in tall grass. Lions do not need stripes like this, because a [[lion|pumpkin|mirror|wallet]] usually lives in open grassland, where its sandy color helps it hide.
> 1 if you shaved a tiger「如果你把老虎的毛剃掉」，前面說老虎的條紋。blanket（毯子）、bucket（水桶）、pencil（鉛筆）都沒有毛和皮膚，後面也不會看到 stripes on its skin。
> 2 use photos to tell... apart「用照片來分辨」，use 是及物動詞，後面接 photos。sleep、arrive、happen 都是不及物動詞，不能接 photos 當受詞。

> 4 hard to notice「很難被注意到」，條紋讓老虎在草叢中不容易被發現。cry（哭）、arrive（抵達）、laugh（笑）放在 hard to ___ in tall grass 都不合語意，也不是用來說明「隱藏」。
> 5 a lion usually lives in open grassland「獅子通常住在空曠的草原」，前面主詞是 Lions。pumpkin（南瓜）、mirror（鏡子）、wallet（皮夾）都不會「住」在草原上躲藏。

## 烏龜不能脫掉殼
In cartoons, a [[turtle|candle|ruler|pillow]] sometimes takes off its shell like a coat and walks around without it. This is just a joke. A real turtle can never leave its shell. The shell is part of its body. It is made of bone, and it is joined to the turtle's backbone and ribs. If you took the shell away, the [[turtle|basket|spoon|kite]] would die. The shell also grows bigger as the animal grows, so a turtle never needs to look for a new one. Hermit crabs are different. They live in empty shells and [[move|cook|sell|paint]] into bigger ones when they grow. Many [[turtles|forks|mirrors|tickets]] can pull their heads and legs into their shells when they are scared. That is why people say a shy person "comes out of their shell" when they become more [[friendly|salty|cloudy|crowded]].

= 林旺是一隻亞洲象，他的一生又長又不平凡。第二次世界大戰期間，他在緬甸替軍隊工作，在森林裡搬運重物。戰爭結束後，他跟著中國軍人來到台灣。1954 年，他搬進當時位在圓山的台北動物園。孩子們都很喜歡他，很多人叫他「林旺爺爺」。1986 年，動物園搬到木柵，林旺和其他動物走過台北的街道，好幾千人出來看。林旺在 2003 年過世時是 86 歲，當時他是全世界動物園裡最老的大象。很多人到動物園和他道別。我阿嬤小時候和林旺拍過一張照片，她到現在還把那張照片掛在牆上。她說：「他是我看過最溫柔的大象。」

## 蛇梯棋來自古印度
Do you know the board game Snakes and Ladders? You roll a die and move along the board. If you land at the bottom of a ladder, you climb up. If you land on a [[snake|pocket|sofa|lamp]]'s head, you slide down to its tail. This simple game is hundreds of years old. It came from ancient India, where it was called Moksha Patam. It was used to teach children about good and bad behavior. The ladders stood for good actions like being kind and honest, and the [[snakes|pencils|blankets|coins]] stood for bad ones like being rude or lazy. In the 1800s, the British [[brought|arrived|slept|happened]] the game to England. Later, it went to the US, where some versions use chutes instead of snakes. Last weekend, I played it with my little cousin. Every time she landed on a [[snake|bowl|fever|towel]], she got [[upset|salty|sunny|thick]] and shouted, "I hate snakes!" But in the end, she won the game.
> 1 land on a snake's head「停在蛇的頭上」，然後滑到蛇尾，遊戲名稱就有 Snakes。pocket（口袋）、sofa（沙發）、lamp（檯燈）都沒有頭和尾巴。
> 2 the snakes stood for bad ones「蛇代表壞的行為」，和前面的 ladders 對比。pencils（鉛筆）、blankets（毯子）、coins（硬幣）都不是棋盤上的圖案。
> 3 brought the game to England「把這個遊戲帶到英國」，bring A to B。arrived、slept、happened 都是不及物動詞，不能接 the game 當受詞。
> 4 landed on a snake「停在蛇的格子上」，所以她很不開心，還大喊討厭蛇。bowl（碗）、fever（發燒）、towel（毛巾）都不是蛇梯棋上的圖案，也和她喊 I hate snakes 不合。
> 5 got upset「變得不開心」，因為一直滑下去。salty（鹹的）、sunny（晴朗的）、thick（厚的）都不能形容人的心情。
+ board game = 桌遊
+ die = 骰子
+ slide = 滑
+ ancient = 古代的
+ chute = 滑道
= 你知道桌遊「蛇梯棋」嗎？你擲骰子，在棋盤上前進。如果停在梯子的底部，就可以爬上去；如果停在蛇的頭上，就要滑到蛇的尾巴。這個簡單的遊戲已經有好幾百年的歷史了。它來自古印度，當時叫做 Moksha Patam，是用來教孩子分辨好行為和壞行為的。梯子代表善良、誠實等好行為，蛇則代表沒禮貌、懶惰等壞行為。1800 年代，英國人把這個遊戲帶到英國。後來它又傳到美國，有些版本用滑道代替蛇。上週末，我和小表妹一起玩。每次她停在蛇的格子上，就很不開心，大喊：「我討厭蛇！」不過最後是她贏了。
