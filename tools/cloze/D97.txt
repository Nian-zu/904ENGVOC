## 不會游泳的小美人魚

## 飛出去的運動鞋

## 會慢跑的機器人

We did not win a prize, but everyone remembered us. Next year, we want to build a robot that can [[hike|fry|mop|iron]] up mountains. And this time, we will keep it far away from water. ## 七星山上的早餐
