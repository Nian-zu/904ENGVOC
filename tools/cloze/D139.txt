In our class, there is a big question: cats or dogs? One day, our teacher asked, "Which do you [[prefer|solve|explore|improve]]?" Half the class shouted "Dogs!" and the other half shouted "Cats!" So the teacher had an idea. "Let's have a debate," she said. "Each side will try to make the other side [[accept|arrive|happen|exist]] their idea." The dog team said dogs are friendly and can help people. The cat team said cats are clean and don't need walks. Then Jenny from the cat team [[introduced|solved|improved|explored]] her cat to the class on a video call. The cat yawned and fell asleep on the keyboard. Everyone laughed. In the end, nobody won. But I [[realized|solved|improved|received]] something important: we don't have to choose. Both are great! The debate was [[worth|polite|honest|thirsty]] the time, because we learned to listen to each other.
> 1 Which do you prefer?「你比較喜歡哪一個？」。solve（解決）、explore（探索）、improve（改善）都不能用來問喜好。
> 2 make + 人 + 原形動詞，accept their idea「接受他們的看法」。arrive、happen、exist 都是不及物動詞，後面不能接受詞。
> 3 introduce A to B「把 A 介紹給 B」，Jenny 把她的貓介紹給全班。solved、improved、explored 都不能表示「介紹」。
> 4 realize something「明白一件事」。solved（解決）、improved（改善）、received（收到）都不能表示「領悟到」。
> 5 be worth the time「值得花時間」。polite（有禮貌的）、honest（誠實的）、thirsty（口渴的）後面都不能接 the time。
+ debate = 辯論
+ side = 一方
+ video call = 視訊通話
+ yawn = 打哈欠
+ keyboard = 鍵盤
= 我們班上有一個大問題：貓還是狗？有一天，老師問：「你們比較喜歡哪一個？」一半的人大喊「狗！」，另一半大喊「貓！」於是老師想到一個點子。「我們來辯論吧，」她說，「每一方都要想辦法讓對方接受自己的看法。」狗隊說狗很友善，還能幫助人。貓隊說貓很乾淨，不用帶出去散步。接著貓隊的 Jenny 用視訊把她的貓介紹給全班。那隻貓打了個哈欠，就趴在鍵盤上睡著了，大家都笑了。最後沒有人贏，但我明白了一件重要的事：我們不必選邊站，兩種都很棒！這場辯論很值得，因為我們學會了傾聽彼此。
