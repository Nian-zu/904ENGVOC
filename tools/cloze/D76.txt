## 珊瑚礁的第四次全球白化

Last month, I helped him [[paint|sneeze|arrive|yawn]] some of the signs.

## 白色沙灘是魚的便便

They bite off pieces of dead coral and rock to eat the tiny algae on them. Their bodies use the algae for food, and the hard coral is ground into fine white sand. Then the fish poop it out. Scientists say a large parrotfish can make hundreds of kilograms of sand every year. On some tropical [[islands|socks|spoons|crayons]], a large part of the white sand on the beaches comes from these fish. Parrotfish also help coral reefs stay healthy, because they eat algae that would cover the corals. + signal = （手機）訊號
+ selfie = 自拍照
+ sail = 航行
= 暑假前的最後一天，導師問了我們一個有趣的問題：「想像你被困在一座無人島上，沒有人、沒有商店，也沒有 Wi-Fi。你只能帶一樣東西，你要帶什麼？」這裡的 desert island 不是指被沙子蓋住的島，而是沒有人住的島。Amy 說她要帶一把刀，這樣就可以切水果，還能在森林裡蓋房子。Kevin 說他要帶手機。老師說：「可是那裡沒有訊號喔。」Kevin 回答：「沒關係，我還是可以在海灘上自拍。」大家都笑了。接著輪到我。我說：「我要帶一艘大船。」老師看著我問：「為什麼是船？」我說：「這樣我就可以開船橫越大海，馬上回家啊！」全班都歡呼起來，老師說那是今天最聰明的答案。

## 132 年前的漂流瓶
In January 2018, Tonya Illman was walking along a [[beach|closet|pillow|drawer]] near Wedge Island in Western Australia. She saw an old glass bottle lying in the sand and picked it up, thinking it might look nice in her house. Inside, there was a rolled-up piece of paper. It was a message written in German, dated June 12, 1886. Experts at the Western Australian Museum checked it carefully. They found that the bottle had been thrown into the Indian [[Ocean|Pillow|Mirror|Towel]] from a German ship named Paula. At that time, German scientists asked sailors to throw bottles into the sea to study how ocean currents move. The note asked the finder to write down where it was found and send it back. The bottle had probably washed up on the [[island|sock|crayon|button]] continent within about a year and then stayed buried in the sand for more than a century. At about 132 years old, it became the oldest known message in a bottle in the world. After reading this story, I wrote a [[letter|fever|cough|noise]] to myself and put it in a box. I will open it ten years [[later|loudly|sadly|softly]].
> 1 walking along a beach「沿著海灘散步」，下一句說瓶子躺在沙子裡。closet、pillow、drawer 都不能沿著散步。
> 2 the Indian Ocean「印度洋」，西澳洲的西邊是印度洋。Pillow、Mirror、Towel 都不能和 Indian 組成地名。
> 3 the island continent「這塊島嶼大陸」，指四面環海的澳洲。sock、crayon、button 都不能形容澳洲。
> 4 wrote a letter to myself「寫一封信給自己」。fever、cough、noise 都不能「寫」，也不能放進盒子裡。
> 5 open it ten years later「十年後打開」，時間 + later 表示「……之後」。loudly（大聲地）、sadly（難過地）、softly（輕柔地）都不能接在 ten years 後面表示時間。
+ Wedge Island = 韋奇島（西澳洲地名）
+ German = 德文的；德國的
+ ocean current = 洋流
+ continent = 大陸；洲
+ century = 世紀；一百年
= 2018 年 1 月，湯雅‧伊爾曼正沿著西澳洲韋奇島附近的海灘散步。她看到一個舊玻璃瓶躺在沙子裡，就撿了起來，想說放在家裡應該很好看。瓶子裡有一張捲起來的紙，是一封用德文寫的訊息，日期是 1886 年 6 月 12 日。西澳洲博物館的專家仔細檢查後發現，這個瓶子是從一艘叫「寶拉號」的德國船上被丟進印度洋的。當時，德國科學家請水手把瓶子丟進海裡，用來研究洋流怎麼流動。紙條上請撿到的人寫下在哪裡撿到，再寄回去。這個瓶子大概在一年內就被沖上這塊島嶼大陸，然後在沙子裡埋了一百多年。它大約有 132 歲，成為世界上已知最古老的漂流瓶。讀了這個故事之後，我寫了一封信給自己，放進一個盒子裡。我要在十年後再打開它。

## 真實版的魯賓遜
Have you read Robinson Crusoe, the story of a man who lives alone on an [[island|sock|crayon|spoon]] for many years? The book came out in 1719, and many people believe it was partly inspired by a real Scottish sailor named Alexander Selkirk. In 1704, Selkirk was on a ship in the Pacific [[Ocean|Pillow|Mirror|Closet]]. He thought the ship was in bad shape and might sink, so he asked to be left on an island about 670 kilometers off the coast of Chile. He thought another ship would come soon. He was wrong. He lived there alone for more than four years. At first, he stayed near the [[beach|ceiling|drawer|towel]] and ate shellfish. Later, he moved into the hills and [[forests|buttons|pillows|socks]] of the island. He hunted wild goats, made clothes from their skins, and read his Bible. In 1709, a British ship finally rescued him. Today, the island is called Robinson Crusoe Island. When I heard this story, I [[realized|sneezed|yawned|arrived]] that real life can be even more surprising than books.
> 1 lives alone on an island「獨自住在一座島上」。sock、crayon、spoon 上面不能住人。
> 2 the Pacific Ocean「太平洋」，智利外海就是太平洋。Pillow、Mirror、Closet 都不能和 Pacific 組成地名。
> 3 stayed near the beach and ate shellfish「待在海灘附近吃貝類」。ceiling（天花板）、drawer（抽屜）、towel（毛巾）附近不會有貝類可以吃。
> 4 the hills and forests of the island「島上的山丘和森林」，他在那裡打獵。buttons、pillows、socks 都不是可以搬進去、打野山羊的地方。
> 5 I realized that …「我體會到……」，realize 後面可以接 that 子句。sneezed、yawned、arrived 都不能接 that 子句。
+ inspire = 給予靈感（inspired 受到啟發）
+ Scottish = 蘇格蘭的
+ shellfish = 貝類
+ goat = 山羊
+ rescue = 救援
= 你讀過《魯賓遜漂流記》嗎？那是一個男人獨自在島上住了很多年的故事。這本書在 1719 年出版，很多人認為它的部分靈感來自一位真實的蘇格蘭水手亞歷山大‧塞爾柯克。1704 年，塞爾柯克在太平洋上的一艘船上。他認為那艘船狀況很差、可能會沉，所以要求讓他留在智利外海大約 670 公里的一座島上。他以為很快就會有別的船來，但他錯了。他獨自在那裡住了四年多。一開始，他待在海灘附近吃貝類；後來，他搬進島上的山丘和森林。他獵野山羊、用羊皮做衣服，還讀他的聖經。1709 年，一艘英國船終於救了他。現在，那座島叫做魯賓遜‧克魯索島。聽到這個故事，我體會到真實人生有時候比書本還要令人驚奇。
