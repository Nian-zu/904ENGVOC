## 鏡子裡的另一個我

## 冰箱門上的便利貼

## 合歡山上的星空

## 舞蹈教室的大鏡子
Every Saturday, Kelly goes to a K-pop dance class. The room has a huge [[mirror|soup|toe|coin]] on one wall, so the students can watch their own moves. They are learning a dance by Moonbeam, a popular girl group. The class is hard, and the room gets very hot. The [[air-conditioner|cookie|elbow|receipt]] is old, so the teacher also turns on two big [[fans|lemons|chins|coupons]] in the corners. After class, Kelly takes a bottle of cold water from the small [[refrigerator|wallet|stair|sock]] by the door. She drinks it all at once. Kelly practices at home every night too. Next year, she wants to join a dance contest. "I'm Moonbeam's biggest [[fan|blanket|mirror|towel]]," she says, "and one day I want to dance on the same stage as them."
> 1 a huge mirror on one wall「一整面牆的大鏡子」，學生才能看自己的動作。soup、toe、coin 都不能看到動作。
> 2 the air-conditioner is old「冷氣很舊」，所以老師還要開電風扇。cookie、elbow、receipt 都不能讓房間變涼。
> 3 turn on two big fans「打開兩台大電扇」。lemons、chins、coupons 都不能「打開」吹風。
> 4 cold water from the refrigerator「從冰箱拿冰水」。wallet、stair、sock 裡面不會有冰水。
> 5 Moonbeam's biggest fan「Moonbeam 最大的粉絲」，這裡 fan 是「粉絲」。blanket、mirror、towel 都不能表示「喜歡某團的人」。
+ move = 動作
+ popular = 受歡迎的
+ contest = 比賽
+ stage = 舞台
= Kelly 每個星期六都去上韓國流行舞蹈課。教室的一面牆上有一大片鏡子，讓學生可以看自己的動作。他們正在學人氣女團 Moonbeam 的舞。課程很累，教室也變得很熱。冷氣很舊，所以老師還會打開角落的兩台大電風扇。下課後，Kelly 從門邊的小冰箱拿出一瓶冰水，一口氣喝光。Kelly 每天晚上也在家練習。明年她想參加舞蹈比賽。「我是 Moonbeam 最大的粉絲，」她說，「總有一天我要跟她們站在同一個舞台上跳舞。」
