Lisa was born in Thailand, and fans loved watching her [[dance|fry|boil|iron]] under the bright signs of her home city. The video got about five million views in just three hours. Some shops on the street had to close during filming, so Lisa paid the shop owners for the business they lost. After the video came out, Yaowarat became a hot spot for tourists. Last winter, my family visited Bangkok. At night, the [[sidewalks|coughs|fevers|soups]] there were full of people eating street food and taking pictures. We had to wait at a busy [[crosswalk|lemon|blanket|sock]] for a long time because there were so many tourists. My sister copied Lisa's pose, and I took a [[photo|sugar|cough|fever]] of her.
> 1 a famous road in Bangkok's Chinatown「曼谷中國城一條有名的路」，耀華力路是一條街道。spoon（湯匙）、towel（毛巾）、candle（蠟燭）都不能在上面拍 MV。

> 3 the sidewalks were full of people「人行道上擠滿了人」。coughs（咳嗽）、fevers（發燒）、soups（湯）都不能讓人在上面吃東西拍照。
> 4 wait at a busy crosswalk「在繁忙的斑馬線前等待」。lemon（檸檬）、blanket（毯子）、sock（襪子）都不是過馬路要等的地方。
> 5 took a photo of her「幫她拍照」。sugar（糖）、cough、fever 都不能「拍」。
+ release = 發行
+ solo = 個人的、單飛的

= 2024 年 6 月 28 日，BLACKPINK 的 Lisa 發行了個人單曲〈Rockstar〉。MV 大部分是在曼谷中國城有名的耀華力路拍攝的。Lisa 在泰國出生，粉絲很喜歡看她在家鄉城市明亮的招牌下跳舞。影片短短三小時就有大約五百萬次觀看。拍攝期間，街上有些店家必須暫停營業，所以 Lisa 補償了店家損失的生意。MV 推出後，耀華力路成了觀光客的熱門景點。去年冬天，我們全家去曼谷玩。晚上那裡的人行道上擠滿了吃小吃、拍照的人。因為遊客太多，我們在一個繁忙的斑馬線前等了好久。我妹妹模仿 Lisa 的姿勢，我幫她拍了一張照片。

## 江南的 K-Star 大道
If you visit Seoul as a K-pop fan, you can take the [[subway|pumpkin|towel|sock]] to Apgujeong Rodeo Station and walk along K-Star Road. This [[road|soup|fever|cough]] is in Gangnam, and it is about one kilometer long. Along the [[sidewalk|lemon|candle|spoon]], there are 17 big bear statues called "GangnamDols." Each bear is painted with the name and logo of a K-pop group, such as BTS, EXO, SHINee, Girls' Generation, and Super Junior. Many fans walk from bear to bear and take photos with their favorite groups. My sister went there last spring. She walked several [[blocks|sugars|fevers|coughs]] in the rain to find the BTS bear. When she finally saw it, she hugged it and asked a friendly stranger to take a [[photo|blanket|sock|lemon]] of them together. She says it is the best picture on her phone.
> 1 take the subway to … Station「搭地鐵到……站」。pumpkin（南瓜）、towel（毛巾）、sock（襪子）都不能搭乘到車站。
> 2 This road is in Gangnam「這條路在江南」，指 K-Star Road。soup（湯）、fever（發燒）、cough（咳嗽）都沒有「一公里長」。
> 3 along the sidewalk「沿著人行道」放著熊熊雕像。lemon（檸檬）、candle（蠟燭）、spoon（湯匙）都不能沿著排列雕像。
> 4 walked several blocks「走了好幾個街區」，block 指「街區」。sugars（糖）、fevers、coughs 都不能用來說走了多遠。
> 5 take a photo of them「幫他們拍照」。blanket（毯子）、sock、lemon 都不能用「拍」來搭配，後面也說是手機裡的照片。
+ Gangnam = 江南（首爾的區）
+ statue = 雕像
+ logo = 標誌

= 如果你是 K-pop 粉絲去首爾玩，可以搭地鐵到狎鷗亭羅德奧站，沿著 K-Star Road 散步。這條路在江南，大約一公里長。人行道旁有 17 隻叫做「江南 Dol」的大熊雕像，每一隻都畫上一個 K-pop 團體的名字和標誌，例如 BTS、EXO、SHINee、少女時代和 Super Junior。很多粉絲一隻一隻地走過去，和喜歡的團體合照。我姊姊去年春天去了那裡。她冒著雨走了好幾個街區，才找到 BTS 的熊。終於看到時，她抱住熊，請一位親切的陌生人幫她們拍合照。她說那是她手機裡最棒的一張照片。

## 雞為什麼要過馬路
In English class, our teacher, Mr. Lin, asked us, "Why did the chicken cross the [[road|spoon|towel|pumpkin]]?" Nobody knew. "To get to the other side!" he said. Some of us laughed, and some of us just looked confused. Then my classmate Ben raised his hand. "I have a better answer. It used the [[crosswalk|blanket|lemon|candle]], because it's a good chicken!" Everyone laughed. Then Kelly said, "No, the chicken didn't cross the road. It walked over the [[bridge|cough|fever|soup]] above the road!" Mr. Lin shook his head and smiled. "Okay, okay. For homework, everyone needs to write a joke in English." The next day, Ben read his joke: "Why did the chicken stay on the [[sidewalk|sugar|sock|spoon]]? Because the light was red!" This time, even Mr. Lin laughed. He gave Ben a perfect [[score|fever|soup|cough]] for his homework.
> 1 cross the road「過馬路」，這是英文裡很有名的冷笑話開頭。spoon（湯匙）、towel（毛巾）、pumpkin（南瓜）都不能「過」到另一邊。
> 2 used the crosswalk「走斑馬線」，守規矩的雞會走行人穿越道。blanket（毯子）、lemon（檸檬）、candle（蠟燭）都不能用來過馬路。
> 3 walked over the bridge above the road「走馬路上方的天橋」。cough（咳嗽）、fever（發燒）、soup（湯）都不能架在馬路上方。
> 4 stay on the sidewalk「待在人行道上」，因為是紅燈不能過馬路。sugar（糖）、sock（襪子）、spoon 都不是等紅燈的地方。
> 5 a perfect score「滿分」。fever、soup、cough 都不能和 perfect 搭配表示作業的成績。
+ confused = 困惑的
+ homework = 回家作業
= 英文課時，林老師問我們：「雞為什麼要過馬路？」沒有人知道。他說：「為了到另一邊去！」我們有些人笑了，有些人一臉困惑。這時我的同學班舉手說：「我有更好的答案。牠走斑馬線，因為牠是一隻乖雞！」大家都笑了。接著凱莉說：「不對，那隻雞沒有過馬路，牠是走馬路上方的天橋！」林老師搖搖頭笑了：「好啦好啦。回家作業，每個人都要寫一個英文笑話。」隔天，班念出他的笑話：「雞為什麼一直待在人行道上？因為是紅燈！」這次連林老師都笑了，他給班的作業打了滿分。

## 三個街區還是三塊積木
Last Saturday, a tourist stopped my little brother and me near the park. "Excuse me, where is the post office?" she asked. "Go straight for three [[blocks|fevers|coughs|soups]], then turn left," I said. My brother is only six, and he looked worried. After she left, he asked, "Why does she need three blocks? Is she going to build something?" He thought I meant three toy [[blocks|noises|coughs|fevers]]! I laughed and explained that "block" can also mean the part of a city between two streets. He thought about it for a moment. "So our home is one block from the [[playground|soup|fever|cough]]?" he asked. "That's right," I said. That night, he took out his toy [[blocks|soups|coughs|sugars]] and built our whole neighborhood on the floor. He even made a little [[bridge|sugar|cough|fever]] over the river near our home.
> 1 go straight for three blocks「直走三個街區」，報路時 block 指「街區」。fevers（發燒）、coughs（咳嗽）、soups（湯）都不能用來指路。
> 2 three toy blocks「三塊玩具積木」，弟弟誤會了 block 的意思，以為要蓋東西。noises（噪音）、coughs（咳嗽）、fevers（發燒）都不是玩具，也不能用來「蓋東西」。
> 3 one block from the playground「離遊樂場一個街區」。soup、fever、cough 都不是地點。
> 4 took out his toy blocks and built…「拿出玩具積木蓋……」。soups（湯）、coughs（咳嗽）、sugars（糖）都不是玩具，不能用來堆出社區模型。
> 5 a little bridge over the river「河上的小橋」。sugar（糖）、cough、fever 都不能架在河上。
+ tourist = 觀光客
+ post office = 郵局
+ neighborhood = 社區、街坊
= 上星期六，一位觀光客在公園附近攔住我和弟弟。她問：「不好意思，請問郵局在哪裡？」我說：「直走三個街區，然後左轉。」我弟弟才六歲，他看起來很擔心。她走後，弟弟問：「她為什麼需要三塊積木？她要蓋東西嗎？」原來他以為我說的是三塊玩具積木！我笑著解釋，block 也可以指城市裡兩條街之間的那一區。他想了一下，問：「所以我們家離遊樂場一個街區？」我說：「沒錯。」那天晚上，他拿出玩具積木，在地板上蓋出我們整個社區，甚至還在我家附近的河上蓋了一座小橋。

## 艾比路上的斑馬線
On August 8, 1969, the four members of the Beatles walked in a line across a [[crosswalk|pumpkin|towel|sock]] outside Abbey Road Studios in London. A photographer stood on a ladder in the middle of the [[road|soup|fever|cough]] and took the picture in just a few minutes. The [[photo|candle|lemon|spoon]] became the cover of their album Abbey Road, and it is now one of the most famous album covers in the world. Today, fans from all over the world come to copy the famous walk. Drivers on this busy street often have to stop and wait while the fans take pictures. In 2010, the British government gave the [[crosswalk|blanket|sugar|drawer]] special protection as an important place in history. My dad loves the Beatles. When we visited London, he made our family walk across it four times until he got the perfect picture. The people waiting on the [[sidewalk|fever|cough|soup]] cheered for us!
> 1 walked across a crosswalk「走過一條斑馬線」，這張照片拍的就是斑馬線。pumpkin（南瓜）、towel（毛巾）、sock（襪子）都不能排成一列走過去。
> 2 in the middle of the road「在馬路中間」，攝影師站在路中央拍照。soup（湯）、fever（發燒）、cough（咳嗽）都沒有「中間」可以站。
> 3 The photo became the cover「這張照片成了封面」。candle（蠟燭）、lemon（檸檬）、spoon（湯匙）都不是前面說的「拍下來的照片」。
> 4 gave the crosswalk special protection「把這條斑馬線列為受保護的地方」。blanket（毯子）、sugar（糖）、drawer（抽屜）都不是歷史地點。
> 5 waiting on the sidewalk「在人行道上等待」的路人。fever、cough、soup 都不是可以站著等的地方。
+ the Beatles = 披頭四（英國樂團）
+ studio = 錄音室
+ ladder = 梯子
+ album = 專輯
+ protection = 保護
= 1969 年 8 月 8 日，披頭四的四位團員排成一列，走過倫敦艾比路錄音室外的斑馬線。一位攝影師站在馬路中間的梯子上，只花幾分鐘就拍下這張照片。這張照片成了他們專輯《艾比路》的封面，現在是全世界最有名的專輯封面之一。今天，世界各地的歌迷都會來模仿這段有名的步伐。這條繁忙街道上的駕駛常常得停下來，等歌迷拍完照。2010 年，英國政府把這條斑馬線列為受保護的歷史地點。我爸爸很愛披頭四。我們去倫敦玩時，他要全家走過斑馬線四次，直到拍出完美的照片。在人行道上等的路人還為我們鼓掌！

## 條條大路通羅馬
There is an old saying: "All [[roads|soups|coughs|fevers]] lead to Rome." It sounds like a joke, but it comes from history. More than 2,000 years ago, the ancient Romans built a huge network of roads across Europe, North Africa, and the Middle East. More than 80,000 kilometers of them were paved with stone. One of the most famous is the Appian Way, which was started in 312 BC. A Roman [[road|spoon|sock|candle]] was built in layers: big stones at the bottom, then smaller stones and sand, and flat stones on top. The middle was a little higher, so rainwater could run off to the sides. The Romans also built strong stone [[bridges|lemons|towels|pumpkins]], and some are still used today. In the city of Pompeii, streets had raised [[sidewalks|sugars|fevers|blankets]] and big stepping stones in the road, so people could cross without stepping in mud. They were like an early [[crosswalk|candle|drawer|spoon]]!
> 1 All roads lead to Rome「條條大路通羅馬」，是有名的諺語，後面也說羅馬人建了很多道路。soups（湯）、coughs（咳嗽）、fevers（發燒）都不能「通往」某個地方。
> 2 A Roman road was built in layers「羅馬道路是一層一層鋪成的」。spoon（湯匙）、sock（襪子）、candle（蠟燭）都不是用石頭和沙子分層鋪成的。
> 3 strong stone bridges「堅固的石橋」，有些到今天還在使用。lemons（檸檬）、towels（毛巾）、pumpkins（南瓜）都不能用石頭蓋成讓人通行。
> 4 raised sidewalks「墊高的人行道」，讓行人走在比馬路高的地方。sugars（糖）、fevers、blankets（毯子）都不是街道的一部分。
> 5 like an early crosswalk「像早期的斑馬線」，踏腳石讓人過馬路。candle、drawer（抽屜）、spoon 都不能幫人過馬路。
+ ancient = 古代的
+ network = 網絡
+ pave = 鋪（路）
+ Appian Way = 阿庇亞古道
+ Pompeii = 龐貝城
= 有一句古老的諺語說：「條條大路通羅馬。」聽起來像玩笑話，其實它來自歷史。兩千多年前，古羅馬人在歐洲、北非和中東各地建造了龐大的道路網，其中用石頭鋪成的就超過八萬公里。最有名的道路之一是西元前 312 年開始興建的阿庇亞古道。羅馬道路是一層一層鋪成的：最底層是大石頭，接著是小石頭和沙子，最上面是平坦的石板。路面中間稍微高一點，雨水才能流到兩旁。羅馬人也蓋了堅固的石橋，有些到今天還在使用。在龐貝城，街道有墊高的人行道，馬路上還有大大的踏腳石，讓人過馬路時不會踩到泥巴。它們就像是早期的斑馬線！
