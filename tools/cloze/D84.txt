## 鄰居爺爺的第一支智慧型手機
Our [[neighbor|pumpkin|eraser|candle]], Grandpa Chen, is 80, and he just got his first smartphone. One day, he knocked on our door and said, "This phone is broken. The map says I'm in a [[parking|fever|cough|sneeze]] lot, but I'm in my living room!" I looked at the screen and laughed. He wasn't looking at the map. He was looking at a photo he took yesterday of the parking lot next to the [[convenience|headache|sneeze|fever]] store! Then he asked me to show him how to take a selfie. He held the phone the [[wrong|honest|polite|lazy]] way and took twenty photos of his own ear. He [[sent|baked|ironed|mopped]] all of them to his family's group chat. Now his grandchildren call him "the ear artist." He thinks the name is funny, and he still sends them a new ear photo every week.
> 1 Our neighbor, Grandpa Chen「我們的鄰居陳爺爺」，80 歲、買手機的要是人。pumpkin、eraser、candle 都不是人。
> 2 a parking lot「停車場」，地圖顯示的地點。fever、cough、sneeze 都不能和 lot 組成場地名稱。
> 3 the convenience store「便利商店」。headache、sneeze、fever 都不能和 store 組成店名。
> 4 the wrong way「拿反了、用錯方式」，所以只拍到耳朵。honest、polite、lazy 都是形容人的個性，不能形容拿手機的方式。
> 5 sent all of them「把照片全部傳出去」，send 的過去式是 sent。baked、ironed、mopped 都不能接照片和 to his group chat。
+ smartphone = 智慧型手機
+ knock = 敲（門）
+ selfie = 自拍
+ group chat = 群組聊天
= 我們的鄰居陳爺爺 80 歲，剛買了他的第一支智慧型手機。有一天，他來敲我們家的門說：「這支手機壞了。地圖說我在停車場，可是我明明在我家客廳！」我看了一下螢幕就笑了。他看的不是地圖，而是他昨天拍的一張便利商店旁邊停車場的照片！接著他請我教他怎麼自拍。結果他把手機拿反了，拍了二十張自己耳朵的照片，還全部傳到家族群組裡。現在他的孫子孫女都叫他「耳朵藝術家」。他覺得這個名字很好笑，到現在每個星期還是會傳一張新的耳朵照片給他們。

## 羅傑斯先生的鄰居們
Mister Rogers' Neighborhood was a children's TV show in the United States that ran from 1968 to 2001. At the start of every show, the host, Fred Rogers, walked in, changed into a [[sweater|fever|receipt|dumpling]] and sneakers, and sang a song that invited the children at home to be his [[neighbors|pumpkins|erasers|candles]]. On the show, he talked about real feelings, like being [[scared|rainy|cloudy|windy]] or angry, and helped children understand them. Many of his sweaters were knitted by his mother, and one of them is now in the Smithsonian museum. Last week, our teacher showed us a short clip of the show. After class, my friend said he would help the old man next door carry water home from the [[convenience|headache|sneeze|fever]] store. I decided to help clean up the [[parking|fever|cough|sneeze]] lot of our building on Saturday.
> 1 changed into a sweater「換上一件毛衣」，change into + 衣服。fever（發燒）、receipt（收據）、dumpling（水餃）都不能穿在身上。
> 2 to be his neighbors「當他的鄰居」，邀請電視機前的小朋友，要用人。pumpkins、erasers、candles 都不是人。
> 3 being scared or angry「害怕或生氣」，scared 是表示感覺的形容詞，和 angry 並列。rainy、cloudy、windy 都是形容天氣，不是人的感覺。
> 4 the convenience store「便利商店」，買水的地方。headache、sneeze、fever 都不能和 store 組成店名。
> 5 the parking lot of our building「我們大樓的停車場」。fever、cough、sneeze 都不能和 lot 組成場地名稱。
+ neighborhood = 社區；鄰里
+ host = 主持人
+ knit = 編織
+ Smithsonian = 史密森尼（美國的博物館）
+ clip = 影片片段
= 《羅傑斯先生的鄰居們》是美國的一個兒童電視節目，從 1968 年播到 2001 年。每一集一開始，主持人弗雷德‧羅傑斯走進來，換上毛衣和運動鞋，唱一首歌邀請電視機前的小朋友當他的鄰居。在節目裡，他會談真實的感受，例如害怕或生氣，並幫助孩子了解這些感受。他的很多件毛衣是他媽媽織的，其中一件現在收藏在史密森尼博物館。上星期，老師給我們看了節目的一小段影片。下課後，我朋友說他要幫隔壁的老先生把水從便利商店提回家。我決定星期六幫忙打掃我們大樓的停車場。

## 走失的小狗麻糬
Last Saturday, our [[neighbor|pumpkin|eraser|candle]], Mrs. Hsu, came to our door in tears. Her little white dog, Mochi, had run away during a walk. We made posters and [[posted|baked|ironed|mopped]] Mochi's photo in our community's LINE group. An hour later, someone sent a message: a small white dog was sitting in front of a [[convenience|headache|sneeze|fever]] store in the [[downtown|thirsty|rude|lazy]] area. We drove there right away. The clerk had given Mochi some water, and Mochi was waiting next to the [[parking|fever|cough|sneeze]] lot, wagging her tail. Mrs. Hsu hugged her and cried again, but this time she was crying with joy. The next day, she baked cookies for the clerk and for us. Now she always checks Mochi's leash before every walk. Mochi is safe at home again, and she still visits the clerk every week.
> 1 our neighbor, Mrs. Hsu「我們的鄰居徐太太」，會哭著來敲門的要是人。pumpkin、eraser、candle 都不是人。
> 2 posted Mochi's photo「把麻糬的照片貼到群組」，post 當動詞是「張貼、發文」。baked、ironed、mopped 都不能接 photo in a group。

> 4 the downtown area「市中心」。thirsty、rude、lazy 都不能形容地區。
> 5 the parking lot「停車場」。fever、cough、sneeze 都不能和 lot 組成場地名稱。

+ community = 社區
+ wag = 搖（尾巴）

= 上星期六，我們的鄰居徐太太哭著來敲我們家的門。她的白色小狗麻糬在散步時跑走了。我們做了尋狗傳單，也把麻糬的照片貼到社區的 LINE 群組。一個小時後，有人傳來訊息：市中心一家便利商店門口坐著一隻白色小狗。我們馬上開車過去。店員已經給麻糬喝了水，麻糬正在停車場旁邊搖著尾巴等著。徐太太抱住牠又哭了，但這次是開心得哭。隔天，她烤了餅乾送給店員和我們。現在她每次散步前都會檢查麻糬的狗鍊。麻糬平安回家了，而且現在每個星期還會去探望那位店員。
