My friend Leo asked her in English, and she answered him too. Lulu can also play a word game with kids. She [[holds|guesses|smells|hosts]] up a picture card, and you have to say the word in English. If you [[guess|smell|hold|host]] right, she dances and plays happy music. Leo got ten right in a row, and Lulu said, "You are a genius!" I think robots like Lulu are very [[useful|national|afraid|delicious]] in busy places like museums, airports, and train stations. But I still like talking to real people more.

## 廟口猜燈謎
At the Lantern Festival, my family went to the temple near our house to [[guess|smell|hold|host]] lantern riddles. The temple [[holds|guesses|smells|wastes]] this event every year, and it is always crowded. The riddles have three [[levels|visitors|decisions|languages]]: easy, medium, and hard. Each riddle is written on a piece of red paper under a lantern. My grandpa is very good at riddles. He [[held|guessed|smelled|hosted]] my hand and took me straight to the hard ones. One riddle said, "It has teeth, but it can't eat. What is it?" I thought for a long time. Grandpa smiled and touched his hair. "A comb!" I shouted. We were right, and we won a very [[useful|national|afraid|delicious]] prize: a big bag of rice. Grandma was happy because she didn't have to buy rice that month. Next year, I want to answer a hard riddle all by myself.
> 1 guess riddles「猜謎」：去廟裡猜燈謎。smell、hold、host 都不能接 lantern riddles 表示猜。
> 2 hold an event「舉辦活動」：廟每年都辦這個活動。guesses、smells、wastes 都不能接 this event 表示舉辦。
> 3 three levels「三個等級」：簡單、中等、困難。visitors、decisions、languages 都不能分成 easy, medium, and hard。
> 4 hold one's hand「牽著某人的手」：爺爺牽著我去最難的區。guessed、smelled、hosted 都不能接 my hand。
> 5 a useful prize「實用的獎品」：一大袋米，奶奶那個月不用買米。national、afraid、delicious 都不能形容這個獎品。
+ Lantern Festival = 元宵節
+ riddle = 謎語
+ temple = 廟
+ comb = 梳子
= 元宵節那天，我們全家到家附近的廟去猜燈謎。這間廟每年都會舉辦這個活動，總是人山人海。燈謎分成三個等級：簡單、中等和困難。每個謎題都寫在燈籠下的一張紅紙上。我爺爺很會猜謎，他牽著我的手直接走到最難的那一區。有一題寫著：「它有牙齒，卻不能吃東西。它是什麼？」我想了好久。爺爺笑著摸了摸自己的頭髮。「梳子！」我大喊。我們答對了，還贏得一個很實用的獎品：一大袋米。奶奶很開心，因為那個月都不用買米了。明年，我想要自己猜中一題難的燈謎。
