## 比太陽表面還熱的閃電
Have you ever wondered why [[thunder|butter|sugar|honey]] is so loud? The answer is heat. According to the US National Weather Service, a flash of lightning can heat the air to about 50,000°F. That is about five times hotter than the surface of the sun! Because the [[temperature|luggage|furniture|homework]] rises so quickly, the air around the lightning expands very fast, almost like an explosion. This sends out a powerful sound wave, and that is the sound we hear. Thunder can usually be heard up to about 16 kilometers away. That is why we sometimes hear it before a [[storm|pencil|cookie|sofa]] reaches our town. In science class, our teacher made a loud sound by popping a paper bag. "Thunder works in a similar way," she said, "but it is much, much [[stronger|sweeter|saltier|softer]]." Now, whenever I hear thunder, I [[remember|forget|borrow|spell]] that paper bag.
> 1 why thunder is so loud「為什麼雷聲那麼大」，後文解釋雷聲的成因。butter、sugar、honey 都不會發出聲音，不能說它很大聲。
> 2 the temperature rises so quickly「溫度上升得很快」，前句說閃電讓空氣變得很熱。luggage、furniture、homework 都不會因為閃電而「升溫」。
> 3 before a storm reaches our town「在暴風雨到達我們城鎮之前」。pencil、cookie、sofa 都不會移動到城鎮來。
> 4 much stronger「（聲波）強得多」，和前文 powerful sound wave 呼應。sweeter（更甜）、saltier（更鹹）、softer（更柔軟、更小聲）都和「雷聲比紙袋大聲很多」相反或不合。
> 5 I remember that paper bag「我會想起那個紙袋」。forget（忘記）意思相反；borrow（借入）、spell（拼字）放進去都不合理。
+ National Weather Service = （美國）國家氣象局
+ surface = 表面
+ expand = 膨脹

## 體溫不一定是 37 度

## 彩虹為什麼有七種顏色？
Have you ever counted the colors of a [[rainbow|fever|cough|noise]]? In Taiwan, we learn that there are seven: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. But in real life, the colors slowly change from one to the next, so there are no clear lines between them. Why seven, then? In the 1600s, the English scientist Isaac Newton used a glass prism to break sunlight into colors. He decided to name seven colors, partly because he wanted them to match the seven notes in music. Since then, many people have described [[rainbows|pianos|tickets|wallets]] with seven colors. Some other cultures name fewer colors. My classmates and I tried to name every color, but we could not [[agree|bake|boil|sweep]] on where green ended and blue began. > 2 described rainbows with seven colors「用七種顏色描述彩虹」。pianos（鋼琴）、tickets（票）、wallets（錢包）都不是用七種光的顏色來描述的東西。

> 4 could not agree on「無法對…意見一致」，agree on 是固定搭配。bake、boil、sweep 都不能接 on 表示「對…有共識」。

+ violet = 紫色
+ Isaac Newton = 牛頓

= 你有沒有數過彩虹的顏色？在台灣，我們學到有七種：紅、橙、黃、綠、藍、靛、紫。但是在真實世界裡，顏色是一點一點慢慢變成下一種的，它們之間沒有清楚的界線。那為什麼是七種呢？在 1600 年代，英國科學家牛頓用玻璃三稜鏡把陽光分成不同的顏色。他決定替七種顏色命名，部分原因是他想讓它們對應音樂裡的七個音。從那時起，很多人就用七種顏色來描述彩虹。有些其他的文化命名的顏色比較少。上星期，一場夏天的暴風雨之後，我從教室看到一道明亮的彩虹。我和同學試著說出每一種顏色，但是我們沒辦法對綠色在哪裡結束、藍色從哪裡開始取得共識。老師笑著說：「這正是牛頓發現的——大自然不會畫線！」
