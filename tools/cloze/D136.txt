## 會越獄的章魚
Inky was an octopus at a sea museum in New Zealand. The workers there said he was the [[smartest|most crowded|most national|most wooden]] animal they had ever seen. He could open jars to get food, and he liked to play with the workers' tools. One night in 2016, a worker [[forgot|borrowed|married|boiled]] to close the top of his tank. Inky climbed out, moved across the floor, and found a small pipe. The pipe went all the way to the sea. When the workers came in the next morning, Inky was gone. They followed the wet marks on the floor and understood what happened. Nobody was angry. "He was very [[clever|delicious|illegal|cheap]]," one worker said. "We'll miss him." Octopuses are not strong, and their bodies are soft. But they are [[gentle|crowded|national|sour]] and curious, and they can learn things quickly. I hope Inky is happy in the ocean now, and I hope he doesn't [[get|spell|marry|invite]] caught again.
> 1 the smartest animal「最聰明的動物」，後文說牠會開罐子。most crowded、most national、most wooden 都不能形容動物的頭腦。（smartest 是 smart 的最高級。）
> 2 forget to V「忘記去做」，員工忘了蓋上水缸。borrow（借）、marry（結婚）、boil（煮沸）都不能接 to close。
> 3 員工稱讚牠很「聰明」clever，自己找到逃脫的路。delicious（好吃的）、illegal（違法的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能這樣稱讚章魚。
> 4 章魚雖然不強壯，但「溫和」又好奇 gentle and curious。crowded、national、sour（酸的）都不能形容章魚的個性。
> 5 get caught「被抓到」。spell（拼字）、marry（結婚）、invite（邀請）都不能和 caught 搭配。
+ octopus = 章魚
+ New Zealand = 紐西蘭
+ tank = 水缸
+ pipe = 管子
+ curious = 好奇的
= Inky 是紐西蘭一間海洋博物館裡的章魚。那裡的員工說，牠是他們見過最聰明的動物。牠會打開罐子拿食物，也喜歡玩員工的工具。2016 年的某天晚上，一位員工忘了把牠的水缸蓋好。Inky 爬了出來，橫越地板，找到一根小管子，那根管子一路通到大海。隔天早上員工進來時，Inky 已經不見了。他們跟著地上的濕痕，才明白發生了什麼事。沒有人生氣。「牠真的很聰明，」一位員工說，「我們會想念牠的。」章魚不強壯，身體又軟，但牠們溫和、好奇，而且學東西很快。我希望 Inky 現在在海裡過得很快樂，也希望牠不會再被抓到。

## 三隻小豬的新故事
Once upon a time, three little pigs left home to build their own houses. The first pig was [[lazy|crowded|national|delicious]]. He wanted to finish quickly, so he built his house with grass. The second pig used sticks, which was a little better. The third pig was [[hard-working|wooden|illegal|sour]]. She spent three weeks building a strong house with bricks. One day, a hungry wolf came. He blew down the grass house and the stick house, and the two pigs [[ran|boiled|spelled|married]] to their sister's brick house. The wolf blew and blew, but the house didn't move. Then he tried to climb down the chimney. But the third pig was also [[clever|crowded|cheap|plastic]]. She put a big pot of hot water under it. In this new story, the wolf doesn't die. He burns his tail, cries, and says sorry. The pigs feel bad for him, and the [[gentle|national|illegal|thick]] third pig puts medicine on his tail. Now the wolf helps them build houses.
> 1 想快點完工，就用草蓋房子，第一隻豬很「懶」lazy。crowded（擁擠的）、national（國家的）、delicious（好吃的）都不能形容豬的個性。
> 2 花三週用磚頭蓋堅固的房子，第三隻豬很「勤奮」hard-working。wooden（木製的）、illegal（違法的）、sour（酸的）都不能形容她的做事態度。
> 3 房子被吹倒，兩隻豬「跑」到姊姊家 ran to...。boil（煮沸）、spell（拼字）、marry（結婚）都不能接 to their sister's house 表示移動。
> 4 她在煙囪下放一鍋熱水，也很「聰明」clever。crowded、cheap（便宜的）、plastic（塑膠的）都不能形容豬的頭腦。
> 5 「溫柔的」第三隻豬幫狼的尾巴擦藥 the gentle third pig。national、illegal、thick（厚的）都不能形容豬的個性。
+ brick = 磚頭
+ wolf = 狼
+ chimney = 煙囪
+ medicine = 藥
= 從前從前，三隻小豬離開家，要蓋自己的房子。第一隻豬很懶，想快點完工，就用草蓋房子。第二隻豬用樹枝，稍微好一點。第三隻豬很勤奮，花了三個星期用磚頭蓋了一棟堅固的房子。有一天，一隻餓狼來了。他吹倒了草屋和木屋，兩隻小豬就跑到姊姊的磚屋去。大野狼吹了又吹，房子卻一動也不動。接著他想從煙囪爬下來，但第三隻豬也很聰明，在煙囪下面放了一大鍋熱水。在這個新故事裡，大野狼沒有死。他燙到尾巴，哭著說對不起。小豬們覺得他很可憐，溫柔的第三隻豬還幫他的尾巴擦藥。現在大野狼幫牠們蓋房子。
