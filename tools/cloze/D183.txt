## 時光機的麻煩
Nobita had a math test tomorrow, but he did not want to study. "Doraemon, can you [[give|learn|start|change]] me a tool for the test?" he asked. Doraemon took out the Time Machine. "You can go to tomorrow and see the questions," he said. Nobita was happy. He jumped into the machine, and it [[started|gave|found|changed]] to shake. A few minutes later, he was in tomorrow's classroom. But there was a big problem: he could not read the test [[because|than|about|just]] it was all in English! He did not know a single word. When he came back, Nobita felt sad. "I [[just|about|than|because]] wanted an easy way," he said. Doraemon smiled. "There is no easy way. If you [[learn|give|start|change]] a few new words every day, tests will not scare you anymore."
> 1 give + 人 + 物「給某人某物」：give me a tool 給我一個道具。learn（學習）、start（開始）、change（改變）都不能接「人＋物」。
> 2 start to + 原形動詞「開始做…」：it started to shake 機器開始搖晃。gave、found、changed 後面不能這樣接 to shake。
> 3 because 引導「原因」：看不懂考卷，因為全是英文。than 用在比較，about 是「關於」，just 是「只是」，都不能連接兩個句子表原因。
> 4 just「只是、只不過」：I just wanted an easy way 我只是想要輕鬆的方法。about、than、because 放在主詞和動詞中間都不通。
> 5 learn new words「學新單字」。give、start、change 和 new words 搭配語意都不通。
+ Nobita = 大雄（《哆啦A夢》的主角）
+ Doraemon = 哆啦A夢
+ Time Machine = 時光機
= 大雄明天有數學考試，但他不想讀書。「哆啦A夢，你可以給我一個考試用的道具嗎？」他問。哆啦A夢拿出時光機。「你可以去明天看看考題。」他說。大雄很開心，他跳進機器，機器開始搖晃。幾分鐘後，他到了明天的教室。但有個大問題：他看不懂考卷，因為考題全是英文！他一個字都不認得。回來之後，大雄很難過。「我只是想要一個輕鬆的方法。」他說。哆啦A夢笑了：「沒有輕鬆的方法。如果你每天學幾個新單字，考試就再也嚇不倒你了。」

## 會說話的手機
Mia got a new phone for her birthday. It could talk! Every morning it said, "Good morning, Mia! It is time to [[get|give|learn|find]] up." At first, Mia loved it. The phone told her funny stories [[about|than|because|just]] animals, and it helped her with her homework. But one day, the phone said, "Mia, you used me for six hours yesterday. That is more [[than|about|because|just]] your sleep time!" Mia was surprised, but she did not want to [[change|give|find|get]] her habits. That night, she could not sleep. She kept thinking about what the phone said. The next evening, she [[gave|started|found|learned]] her phone to her mom at nine o'clock. Now she sleeps better, and the phone is happy too.
> 1 get up「起床」是固定片語。give up 是「放棄」，learn up、find up 都不是片語；鬧鐘叫人「起床」要用 get up。
> 2 stories about animals「關於動物的故事」，about 表示「關於」。than、because、just 都不能接在名詞後面說明內容。
> 3 more … than …「比…更多」：六小時比你睡覺的時間還多。前面有 more，就要用 than。
> 4 change her habits「改變她的習慣」。give、find、get 接 habits 意思不通。
> 5 give + 物 + to + 人「把某物交給某人」：她九點把手機交給媽媽。started、found、learned 都不能用「物 to 人」的句型。
+ habit = 習慣
= Mia 生日收到一支新手機，它會說話！每天早上它都說：「早安，Mia！該起床了。」一開始 Mia 很喜歡它。手機會講關於動物的好笑故事，也會幫她寫功課。但有一天，手機說：「Mia，你昨天用了我六個小時，比你睡覺的時間還多！」Mia 很驚訝，但她不想改變自己的習慣。那天晚上，她睡不著，一直想著手機說的話。隔天晚上九點，她把手機交給了媽媽。現在她睡得比較好，手機也很開心。

## 樹上的貓
"Where is Coco?" Ben shouted. His cat was not in the house. He looked under the bed, behind the sofa, and in the kitchen, but he could not [[find|give|learn|change]] her. Then he ran outside [[because|than|about|just]] he heard a sound from the tree. There she was, at the top of the tree! "Come down, Coco!" he said. But Coco did not move. She was too scared. Ben's dad came out with a ladder. He climbed up and [[got|gave|learned|started]] Coco down safely. Ben hugged his cat. "You are [[just|than|because|about]] like me," he laughed. "You love to climb, but you are afraid of coming down." From that day on, Ben always closes the windows. He does not want to [[start|give|learn|find]] another long search.
> 1 find「找到」：到處找都找不到貓 could not find her。give、learn、change 都不是「找到」的意思。
> 2 because 說明原因：他跑出去，因為聽到樹上有聲音。than、about、just 都不能連接兩個句子表原因。
> 3 get + 人／動物 + down「把…弄下來」：爸爸把 Coco 安全地抱下來。gave、learned、started 不能這樣用。
> 4 just like me「就跟我一樣」，just 加強語氣。than 用在比較級後面，because、about 放在這裡都不通。
> 5 start another search「再開始一次搜尋」。give、learn、find 接 search 意思不通（find a search 不合理）。
+ Coco = 可可（貓的名字）
+ ladder = 梯子
+ search = 搜尋、尋找
= 「可可在哪裡？」Ben 大喊。他的貓不在家裡。他找了床底下、沙發後面和廚房，但都找不到牠。接著他跑到外面，因為他聽到樹上有聲音。牠就在那裡，在樹的最頂端！「下來，可可！」他說。但可可動也不動，牠太害怕了。Ben 的爸爸拿著梯子出來，爬上去把可可安全地抱了下來。Ben 抱住他的貓，笑著說：「你就跟我一樣，喜歡往上爬，卻怕下來。」從那天起，Ben 總是會把窗戶關好，他可不想再開始一次漫長的搜尋。

## 學校的新規定
Our school has a new rule: students cannot use phones in class. Many students were not happy [[about|than|just|because]] it. "How can we look up words now?" asked Jason. Our teacher, Ms. Lin, had an idea. She [[gave|learned|changed|started]] every student a small paper dictionary. "Try this," she said. "Paper dictionaries are slower [[than|about|because|just]] phones, but you will remember the words better." At first, it was hard. But soon we [[learned|gave|got|changed]] how to use them quickly. Some students even read the dictionaries for fun! Now Jason says, "The rule was not bad. It [[changed|found|gave|started]] the way I study."
> 1 be happy about + 事情「對某事感到開心」：不滿意這個規定 not happy about it。because 後面要接句子，than、just 都不能接在 happy 後面。
> 2 give + 人 + 物：她給每個學生一本小字典。learned、changed、started 不能接「人＋物」。
> 3 slower than phones「比手機慢」，比較級 slower 後面接 than。
> 4 learn how to + 原形動詞「學會怎麼做」：我們很快就學會怎麼使用字典。gave how、got how、changed how 都不通。
> 5 change the way I study「改變我讀書的方式」。found、gave、started 接 the way I study 意思不通。
+ look up = 查（字典）
+ dictionary = 字典
= 我們學校有一條新規定：上課不能用手機。很多學生對這條規定不滿意。「那我們現在要怎麼查單字？」Jason 問。我們的林老師想到一個辦法，她給每個學生一本小小的紙本字典。「試試看，」她說，「紙本字典比手機慢，但你會把單字記得更牢。」一開始很難，但我們很快就學會怎麼快速使用字典。有些同學甚至把字典當成好玩的書來讀！現在 Jason 說：「這個規定不壞，它改變了我讀書的方式。」

## 機器人老師
In the year 2050, a robot teacher [[started|found|gave|learned]] working at Sunny Junior High. Its name was Max. Max knew everything [[about|than|because|just]] math, science, and history. Students could ask it any question, and it answered in one second. But the students soon noticed a problem. Max was smart, but it could not tell jokes. Its classes were more boring [[than|about|just|because]] a long bus ride. So the students decided to help. Every day, they [[gave|learned|changed|started]] Max a funny story. Slowly, Max learned to laugh. One day, it told its first joke: "Why did the computer go to the doctor? [[Because|About|Than|Just]] it had a virus!" Everyone laughed, and Max smiled a big robot smile.
> 1 start + V-ing「開始做某事」：機器人老師開始在學校工作。found working、gave working、learned working 都不對。
> 2 know everything about …「知道關於…的一切」，about 表示「關於」。
> 3 more boring than …「比…更無聊」：課比坐很久的公車還無聊。前面有 more，後面接 than。
> 4 give + 人 + 物：學生每天給 Max 一個好笑的故事。learned、changed、started 不能接「人＋物」。
> 5 回答 Why 的問題用 Because：因為它中毒了（有病毒）！About、Than、Just 都不能用來回答原因。
+ robot = 機器人
+ notice = 注意到
+ virus = 病毒（電腦病毒）
= 西元 2050 年，一位機器人老師開始在陽光國中工作，它的名字叫 Max。Max 什麼都懂：數學、自然和歷史。學生問它任何問題，它一秒鐘就能回答。但學生很快就發現一個問題：Max 很聰明，卻不會講笑話，它的課比坐很久的公車還無聊。於是學生決定幫忙。他們每天給 Max 一個好笑的故事。慢慢地，Max 學會了笑。有一天，它講了第一個笑話：「電腦為什麼要去看醫生？因為它中毒了！」大家都笑了，Max 也露出大大的機器人笑容。

## 新髮型
Lily wanted to [[change|learn|find|give]] her look before the school trip. She went to a hair salon and asked for short hair. When she looked in the mirror, she almost cried. Her hair was much shorter [[than|about|because|just]] she wanted! That night, she hid in her room. The next morning, she [[just|than|about|because]] put on a hat and went to school. To her surprise, her friends loved her new hair. "You look like a movie star!" said Emma. Lily took off her hat and smiled. That day, she [[learned|gave|started|changed]] that other people do not look at us as carefully as we think. Also, hair always grows back. There is no need to worry [[about|than|just|because]] small things!
> 1 change her look「改變她的外型」。learn、find、give 接 her look 意思不通。
> 2 shorter than she wanted「比她想要的還短」，比較級 shorter 後面接 than。
> 3 just「就只是」：她就只是戴上帽子去上學。than、about、because 放在主詞和動詞中間都不通。
> 4 learn that + 句子「明白、學到…」：她明白別人不會像我們想的那樣仔細看我們。gave that、started that、changed that 都不通。
> 5 worry about …「擔心…」是固定搭配。
+ hair salon = 美髮店
+ mirror = 鏡子
+ grow back = （頭髮）長回來
= Lily 想在校外教學前改變一下外型。她去美髮店，要求剪短髮。看到鏡子時，她差點哭出來：頭髮比她想要的短多了！那天晚上，她躲在房間裡。隔天早上，她就只是戴上帽子去上學。沒想到，朋友們都很喜歡她的新髮型。「你看起來像電影明星！」Emma 說。Lily 拿下帽子笑了。那天她明白了：別人不會像我們以為的那樣仔細看我們。而且頭髮總會長回來，不用為小事擔心！
