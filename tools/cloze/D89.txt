## 地圖上的格陵蘭被放大了

## 不照比例畫的地鐵圖

So Beck drew a [[map|sugar|candle|towel]] with straight lines that only went in a few [[directions|spoons|lemons|blankets]], and he put the stations at almost equal distances. At first, the company wasn't sure about it, but riders loved it. Today, almost every [[subway|pumpkin|sock|fever]] map in the world, including the Taipei MRT map, uses Beck's idea. The next time you look at a [[map|cough|soup|sugar]] at an MRT station, remember Harry Beck!
