## 奶奶的百褶裙
My grandma loves to dance. Every morning, she dances with her friends in the park near our home. Last month, their group joined a dance contest for old people. Grandma wanted to look her best, so she took out an old blue pleated [[skirt|lemon|spoon|bowl]] from her closet. She wore it when she was young! It still fit her well. She also bought new white [[socks|soups|grapes|menus]] and a pair of soft dance [[sneakers|noodles|knives|throats]]. For a week, she practiced every day. On the day of the contest, I went to [[cheer|bake|spell|marry]] for her. When the music started, her skirt moved like a flower. Grandma and her friends danced so well that they won second prize. She was so [[proud|wooden|empty|salty]] of herself. With a big smile, she said, "Age is just a number!"
