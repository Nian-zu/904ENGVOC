## 會畫畫的 AI
Last night, my little brother asked an AI app to [[paint|cry|forget|welcome]] a picture of a cat eating noodles on the moon. The picture appeared in just a few seconds. It was [[almost|ago|away|least]] perfect, but if you looked closely, the cat had six legs and two tails! We laughed until our stomachs hurt. "Using AI is easy, but drawing a picture by yourself is more [[difficult|famous|popular|free]], and that's why it feels better when you finish." So my brother took out his crayons and spent the whole evening drawing his own moon cat. It had only four legs and one tail, but its noodles looked more like worms. ## 馬祖的藍眼淚
Last summer, my family took a boat to Matsu to see the famous "blue tears." Don't worry, nobody was crying! The blue tears are tiny sea animals that shine blue at night. Each one is [[almost|ago|away|least]] too small to see, but millions of them together can light up the whole beach. Finding them was [[difficult|famous|popular|free]] because they don't appear every night. It depends on the weather and the sea. On our first two nights, we saw nothing. Our guide said, "You're lucky to be here now. A week [[ago|almost|away|still]], there were none at all." On the third night, the waves finally began to glow. Every time the water touched the sand, it shone like stars from outer [[space|size|government|paint]]. My sister and I ran along the beach, and our footprints glowed blue behind us. I will never [[forget|cry|welcome|marry]] that night.
> 1 almost too small to see「小到幾乎看不見」。ago、away、least 都不能放在 too small 前面修飾。
> 2 difficult「困難的」：藍眼淚不是每晚都出現，很難找到。famous、popular、free 都和「要看天氣和海況」接不起來。
> 3 時間 + ago「…以前」：一週前完全沒有。almost、away、still 都不能接在 a week 後面。
> 4 outer space「外太空」：像外太空的星星一樣閃亮。size、government、paint 都不能接在 outer 後面。
> 5 never forget + 物「永遠忘不了…」：我永遠忘不了那一夜。cry、welcome、marry that night 都不通。

+ blue tears = 藍眼淚

= 去年暑假，我們全家搭船去馬祖看有名的「藍眼淚」。別擔心，沒有人在哭！藍眼淚其實是會在夜裡發出藍光的微小海洋生物。每一隻都小到幾乎看不見，但幾百萬隻聚在一起，就能照亮整片海灘。要看到牠們很難，因為牠們不是每天晚上都出現，要看天氣和海況。前兩個晚上，我們什麼都沒看到。導遊說：「你們現在來很幸運，一個星期前完全沒有。」第三天晚上，海浪終於開始發光。每次海水碰到沙灘，就像外太空的星星一樣閃亮。我和妹妹沿著海灘奔跑，我們的腳印在身後發出藍光。我永遠忘不了那一夜。

## 妹妹的向日葵
My little sister spent the whole afternoon [[painting|crying|forgetting|welcoming]] a picture for Mom's birthday. It was a big yellow sunflower, [[almost|ago|away|least]] as tall as she was. She put it on the floor to dry and went to wash her hands. Just then, our dog Lucky ran into the room with muddy feet and walked right across the picture. When my sister came back, she saw the brown footprints and began to [[cry|welcome|marry|wear]]. I wanted to help, and suddenly I had an idea. "Look! Lucky's paws are the perfect [[size|space|government|history]] for little flowers!" We put Lucky's feet into yellow and orange paint and pressed them around the sunflower. Soon there was a whole garden of tiny paw flowers. That evening, Mom said it was the most [[special|difficult|dangerous|wrong]] gift she had ever received. Lucky got a bath, and he wasn't happy about it.
> 1 spend + 時間 + V-ing「花時間做…」：妹妹花了一下午畫畫。crying a picture、forgetting a picture、welcoming a picture 都不通。
> 2 almost as tall as…「幾乎跟…一樣高」。ago、away、least 都不能放在 as tall as 前面。
> 3 begin to cry「開始哭」：畫被狗踩髒了，妹妹哭了。welcome、marry、wear 後面沒有受詞，放在這裡都不通。

+ muddy = 沾滿泥巴的

= 我妹妹花了一整個下午畫一幅畫，要送給媽媽當生日禮物。那是一朵黃色的大向日葵，幾乎跟她一樣高。她把畫放在地上晾乾，然後去洗手。就在那時，我們家的狗 Lucky 帶著沾滿泥巴的腳跑進房間，直接從畫上走過去。妹妹回來看到棕色的腳印，哭了起來。我想幫忙，突然想到一個點子：「你看！Lucky 的腳掌大小剛好可以印小花！」我們把 Lucky 的腳沾上黃色和橘色的顏料，壓在向日葵周圍。很快就出現了一整座小腳印花園。那天晚上，媽媽說這是她收過最特別的禮物。Lucky 被抓去洗澡，牠一點都不開心。
