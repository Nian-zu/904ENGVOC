## 音樂老師的梯子

Nobody did. "Because today, we are going to sing the high notes!" The whole class groaned. Then he asked, "What kind of music do balloons hate?" After a long silence, he said, "Pop music!" This time, a few students laughed. By the end of the semester, we were used to his jokes. We even started a joke [[club|weather|receipt|noise]] at lunch to make our own. On the last day, we gave him a small trophy we made in [[art|pumpkin|fever|cough]] class. It said, "World's Worst Joke Teacher." He loved it. He told us that he had wanted to be a comedian when he was at [[university|sugar|noise|weather]]. "But after I [[graduated|borrowed|baked|boiled]], I chose music," he said. "My jokes were too bad for TV!"

## 一張白紙的畫
In art class, Ms. Wang asked us to draw a cow eating grass. We had thirty minutes. Most of us drew big cows, green fields, and blue skies. But when Ms. Wang [[checked|boiled|married|arrived]] Peter's paper, it was almost empty. "Peter, where is the grass?" she asked. "The cow ate it," Peter said. "Then where is the cow?" "Well, there was no more grass, so the cow went home." The whole class laughed, and even Ms. Wang smiled. She gave Peter a C, but she also put his "picture" on the wall outside the [[art|fever|cough|sugar]] room with a sign that said, "The Funniest Picture of the Year." Later, Peter joined the school drama [[club|weather|noise|receipt]], and now he writes funny plays. His big brother is studying [[music|pumpkin|fever|cough]] at a [[university|sugar|weather|noise]] in Tainan, and the two of them are making a short musical together. I hope it has more than just empty paper.
