He [[washed|arrived|cried|slept]] everything there, waited for the dryer, and [[carried|arrived|cried|slept]] the bags home. The next morning, I put on my school uniform and walked to school. In class, my friend Amy looked at me and laughed. "Why does your shirt say Lin Mei-ling?" she asked. I looked down. The name on my shirt wasn't mine! Dad had picked up the wrong bag at the laundromat. After school, we went back. The laundromat was [[clean|hungry|honest|angry]] and quiet, and a girl was waiting there with her mother. She was holding a bag of our clothes. "I wore your [[jacket|fever|cough|weather]] to school today," she said, and we both laughed. Dad said sorry many times. Now he writes our family name on every bag before he goes to the laundromat.
> 1 took our laundry to the laundromat「把要洗的衣服拿去自助洗衣店」。village、noise、distance 都不能「拿去」洗。
> 2 washed everything「把所有衣服洗好」。arrived、cried、slept 都不能接受詞 everything。
> 3 carried the bags home「把袋子提回家」。arrived、cried、slept 都是不及物動詞，不能接 the bags。
> 4 The laundromat was clean and quiet「洗衣店又乾淨又安靜」。hungry、honest、angry 都是形容人的，不能形容店。
> 5 wore your jacket「穿了你的外套」，因為拿錯袋子。fever、cough、weather 都不能穿。
+ laundromat = 自助洗衣店
+ dryer = 烘衣機
= 上星期我們家的洗衣機壞了，所以爸爸把要洗的衣服拿到公寓附近的自助洗衣店。他在那裡把所有衣服洗好，等烘衣機烘完，再把袋子提回家。隔天早上，我穿上學校制服走路上學。上課時，我的朋友 Amy 看著我笑了。「為什麼你的衣服上寫著林美玲？」她問。我低頭一看，衣服上的名字不是我的！原來爸爸在洗衣店拿錯袋子了。放學後我們回到那裡。洗衣店又乾淨又安靜，一個女生和她媽媽正在那裡等，手上拿著一袋我們的衣服。「我今天穿你的外套去上學了。」她說，我們兩個都笑了。爸爸道歉了好多次。現在他去洗衣店之前，都會先在每個袋子上寫我們家的姓。

## 沙灘上的遙控器
Last Sunday, our class joined a beach clean-up at Baishawan. When we arrived, the beach looked beautiful from far away. But when we got closer, we saw plastic bottles, fishing nets, and cigarette ends in the sand. Our teacher gave each of us gloves and a bag. "Let's [[clean|arrive|cry|lie]] this beach together," she said. We worked for three hours. I found [[garbage|weather|history|fever]] everywhere: a shoe, a toothbrush, and even a TV remote! Some volunteers used brooms to [[sweep|arrive|cry|rise]] the sand off the walking path. At the end, we had forty big bags. Before we went home, we [[washed|arrived|cried|slept]] our hands at the beach shower. I took the remote home as a joke and gave it to Dad. "Now you can change the channel on the sea," I said. He laughed. Our shoes and clothes were full of sand, so Mom had to do a lot of [[laundry|village|noise|distance]] that night.
> 1 Let's clean this beach「我們一起把海灘清乾淨」，let's 後接原形動詞。arrive、cry、lie 都不能接受詞 this beach。
> 2 found garbage everywhere「到處都找到垃圾」，後面舉例鞋子、牙刷、遙控器。weather、history、fever 都不能被撿到。
> 3 sweep the sand off the path「把沙子從步道上掃掉」。arrive、cry、rise 都不能接受詞 the sand。
> 4 washed our hands「洗手」。arrived、cried、slept 都不能接受詞 our hands。
> 5 do a lot of laundry「洗很多衣服」，因為衣服都是沙。village、noise、distance 都不能和 do 組成家事。
+ Baishawan = 白沙灣
+ clean-up = 清潔活動
+ fishing net = 漁網
+ cigarette end = 菸蒂
+ remote = 遙控器
= 上星期天，我們班參加了白沙灣的淨灘活動。剛到的時候，從遠處看海灘很美，但走近一看，沙子裡有塑膠瓶、漁網和菸蒂。老師發給每個人手套和袋子。「我們一起把這片海灘清乾淨吧。」她說。我們做了三個小時，我到處都撿到垃圾：一隻鞋、一支牙刷，甚至還有一個電視遙控器！有些志工用掃把把步道上的沙子掃掉。最後我們裝了四十大袋。回家前，我們在海灘的沖水處洗了手。我把遙控器帶回家當玩笑送給爸爸。「現在你可以幫大海轉台了。」我說。他笑了。我們的鞋子和衣服全是沙，所以那天晚上媽媽得洗好多衣服。

## 手忙腳亂的遊戲廚房
My brother and I love a video game called Crazy Kitchen. In the game, we work in a busy restaurant together. One player cooks, and the other player [[washes|arrives|cries|sleeps]] the plates. If there are no clean plates, the customers get [[angry|national|modern|foreign]] and leave. Last night, we played the hardest level. There was water all over the floor, and our characters kept falling down. "Quick! [[Mop|Arrive|Cry|Happen]] the floor!" I shouted. My brother ran to get the mop, but he dropped a cake on the way. Then a rat ran into the kitchen! "Who [[cleans|arrives|sleeps|cries]] this place?" my brother joked. We failed the level five times. After the game, Mom walked in and looked at the real kitchen. Our snack bowls and cups were all over the table. "You two are great at cooking in games," she said. "Now come and [[wash|cry|arrive|rise]] these real bowls."
> 1 washes the plates「洗盤子」，主詞 the other player 第三人稱單數。arrives、cries、sleeps 都不能接受詞 the plates。
> 2 get angry「生氣」，沒有乾淨盤子客人就生氣離開。national、modern、foreign 都不能用來形容客人的情緒。
> 3 Mop the floor!「快拖地！」祈使句用原形動詞。Arrive、Cry、Happen 都不能接受詞 the floor。
> 4 Who cleans this place?「這地方是誰在打掃的啊？」開玩笑說廚房很髒。arrives、sleeps、cries 都不能接受詞 this place。
> 5 come and wash these bowls「過來洗這些碗」。cry、arrive、rise 都不能接受詞 these bowls。
+ character = 角色
+ rat = 老鼠
= 我和哥哥很愛一款叫《瘋狂廚房》的電玩遊戲。在遊戲裡，我們一起在一家忙碌的餐廳工作：一個人煮菜，另一個人洗盤子。如果沒有乾淨的盤子，客人就會生氣離開。昨天晚上，我們玩最難的一關。地上到處都是水，我們的角色一直跌倒。「快！拖地！」我大喊。哥哥跑去拿拖把，結果半路把一個蛋糕掉在地上。接著一隻老鼠跑進廚房！「這地方到底是誰在打掃的？」哥哥開玩笑說。我們那一關失敗了五次。玩完遊戲，媽媽走進來看看真正的廚房——我們的零食碗和杯子擺滿了桌子。「你們兩個在遊戲裡很會煮菜嘛，」她說，「現在過來洗這些真正的碗。」
