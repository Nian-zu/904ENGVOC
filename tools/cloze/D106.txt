## 愛管閒事的鸚鵡
My grandma has a parrot named Lucky. Lucky can say many things, but his favorite sentence is "Go [[clean|arrive|cry|sleep]] your room!" He learned it from Grandma, who says it to my cousin every day. Last Sunday, I visited Grandma. While I was playing games on my phone, Lucky flew onto my [[shoulder|weather|fever|history]] and shouted, "Go clean your room! Go clean your room!" "This isn't even my room!" I said. But Lucky didn't stop. So I picked up a broom and started [[sweeping|sleeping|crying|arriving]] the floor. Lucky watched me carefully. Then he said, "Good boy!" Grandma laughed so hard that she had to sit down. After that, I [[washed|cried|arrived|slept]] Lucky's water bowl and gave him some fresh water. Grandma gave me a big [[piece|fever|cough|noise]] of cake as a thank-you. Now, every time I hear someone say "Go clean your room," I think of that bossy little bird.
> 1 Go clean your room!「去整理你的房間！」go 後面直接接原形動詞。arrive、cry、sleep 都不能接受詞 your room。
> 2 flew onto my shoulder「飛到我的肩膀上」。weather、fever、history 都不是鳥能停的地方。
> 3 started sweeping the floor「開始掃地」。sleeping、crying、arriving 都不能接受詞 the floor。
> 4 washed Lucky's water bowl「洗 Lucky 的水碗」。cried、arrived、slept 都不能接受詞。
> 5 a piece of cake「一塊蛋糕」。fever、cough、noise 都不能用來表示蛋糕的單位。

= 我奶奶養了一隻叫 Lucky 的鸚鵡。Lucky 會說很多話，但牠最喜歡的一句是：「去整理你的房間！」那是跟奶奶學的，因為奶奶每天都對我表哥這樣說。上星期天我去看奶奶，正在玩手機遊戲時，Lucky 飛到我肩膀上大叫：「去整理你的房間！去整理你的房間！」「這根本不是我的房間！」我說。但 Lucky 不停地叫，我只好拿起掃把開始掃地。Lucky 仔細地看著我，然後說：「好孩子！」奶奶笑到得坐下來。之後我洗了 Lucky 的水碗，幫牠換了新鮮的水。奶奶給我一大塊蛋糕當謝禮。現在每次聽到有人說「去整理你的房間」，我就會想起那隻愛指使人的小鳥。
