## 七十歲才出國的阿嬤

## 想出國比賽的桌球少女
Mei is the best table tennis player at our school. She practices for three hours every day, so she [[seldom|abroad|ago|else]] has time to watch TV or go shopping. Her dream is to play [[abroad|ago|either|else]] in a big international game. Last year, she lost the final game in Taiwan by only two points. "I'm not [[lucky|modern|delicious|national]]," she cried. Her coach said, "Luck is not the point. You need to work on your backhand." So this year, Mei practiced harder [[than|because|about|just]] ever. She watched videos of famous players and practiced her backhand for an extra hour every night. This time, she won the final! Next month, she will fly to Japan for her first international game. "This is [[such|very|too|so]] an exciting moment," she said, holding her prize. "But my work is just beginning."
> 1 seldom「很少」：每天練三小時，很少有時間看電視。abroad、ago、else 都不能放在主詞和動詞之間。
> 2 play abroad「到國外比賽」。ago、either、else 都不能接在 play 後面表示地點。
> 3 I'm not lucky.「我運氣不好」：只差兩分輸掉。modern、delicious、national 都不能形容自己的運氣。
> 4 比較級 + than ever「比以往更…」：練得比以前更努力。because、about、just 都不能接在比較級後面。
> 5 such an exciting moment「這麼令人興奮的時刻」。very、too、so 都不能放在 an 前面。
+ table tennis = 桌球
+ international = 國際的
+ coach = 教練
+ backhand = 反手拍
= 小美是我們學校最厲害的桌球選手。她每天練習三個小時，所以很少有時間看電視或逛街。她的夢想是出國參加大型國際比賽。去年，她在台灣的決賽只差兩分輸了。「我運氣不好。」她哭著說。她的教練說：「重點不是運氣，你需要加強反手拍。」所以今年，小美練得比以前更努力。她看有名選手的影片，每天晚上多練一小時反手拍。這一次，她贏得了決賽！下個月，她就要飛到日本參加她的第一場國際比賽。「這真是令人興奮的時刻，」她拿著獎盃說，「但我的努力才剛開始。」
