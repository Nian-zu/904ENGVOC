## 和阿公下象棋

At first, I thought it was a [[boring|thirsty|illegal|wet]] game for old people. But I soon found that every move was important. If I didn't [[concentrate|decorate|celebrate|marry]], Grandpa took my pieces one by one. He never let me win. "Winning is not the point," he said. "Thinking is." Last week, I finally beat him for the first time. I was so [[excited|tidy|illegal|thick]] that I jumped up and shouted. All the old men around us laughed and clapped. Grandpa smiled and said, "You learn [[fast|wet|cheap|thick]]. Next time, I'll play harder." Now I go to the park with him every Saturday. I think Chinese chess is the most [[exciting|excited|illegal|tidy]] game in the world.
> 1 a boring game「無聊的遊戲」，後面用 But 轉折說其實每一步都很重要。thirsty（口渴的）、illegal（違法的）、wet（濕的）都不能和 But 形成對比。
> 2 concentrate「專心」，不專心就會被吃掉棋子。decorate（裝飾）、celebrate（慶祝）、marry（嫁娶）都不能說明為什麼會輸。
> 3 so excited that I jumped up「興奮到跳起來」。tidy（整齊的）、illegal（違法的）、thick（厚的）都不能形容人的心情。
> 4 learn fast「學得快」，fast 當副詞修飾 learn。wet（濕的）、cheap（便宜的）、thick（厚的）都不能形容學習的速度。
> 5 the most exciting game「最刺激的遊戲」，形容事物用 -ing。excited 是形容人的感覺，不能形容遊戲；illegal（違法的）、tidy（整齊的）也不合。
+ Chinese chess = 象棋
+ move = 一步棋
+ beat = 打敗
= 我爺爺很愛下象棋。每個星期六下午，他都坐在公園的大樹下，和老朋友們下棋。今年夏天，他開始教我。一開始，我以為這是老人家玩的無聊遊戲。但我很快就發現每一步都很重要。只要我不專心，爺爺就會把我的棋子一顆一顆吃掉。他從來不讓我贏。「輸贏不是重點，」他說，「思考才是。」上星期，我終於第一次贏了他。我興奮得跳起來大叫，旁邊的老爺爺們都笑著鼓掌。爺爺笑著說：「你學得很快。下次我要認真下了。」現在我每個星期六都跟他去公園。我覺得象棋是世界上最刺激的遊戲。

## 和太空人說話的十分鐘
Last month, our school had an amazing [[opportunity|semester|garbage|uniform]]. We got to talk to an astronaut on the International Space Station! Only ten students could ask questions, and I was one of them. I was so [[excited|tidy|illegal|cheap]] that I couldn't sleep the night before. On the big day, we sat in the school hall around a big radio. The [[communication|semester|headache|uniform]] with the space station lasted only ten minutes because the station flies around the Earth very fast. When it was my turn, I asked, "What do you miss most about the Earth?" The astronaut laughed and said, "Rain, and my mom's cooking!" Another student asked how astronauts [[brush|marry|borrow|spell]] their teeth. She said they use special toothpaste and then swallow it. Everyone shouted, "Eww!" It was the shortest but best [[lesson|ticket|bill|pocket]] I've ever had.
> 1 had an amazing opportunity「有一個很棒的機會」，可以和太空人說話。semester（學期）、garbage（垃圾）、uniform（制服）都不能說「很棒，可以和太空人說話」。
> 2 so excited that I couldn't sleep「興奮到睡不著」。tidy（整齊的）、illegal（違法的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能形容人的心情。
> 3 The communication with the space station「和太空站的通訊」，只維持十分鐘。semester（學期）、headache（頭痛）、uniform（制服）都不能和 with the space station 搭配。
> 4 brush their teeth「刷牙」，後面說用特殊的牙膏。marry（嫁娶）、borrow（借）、spell（拼字）都不能接 their teeth。
> 5 the best lesson「最棒的一堂課」。ticket（票）、bill（帳單）、pocket（口袋）都不能說成「上過」的東西。
+ astronaut = 太空人
+ International Space Station = 國際太空站
+ radio = 無線電
+ toothpaste = 牙膏
+ swallow = 吞下
= 上個月，我們學校有一個很棒的機會：我們可以和國際太空站上的太空人說話！只有十個學生能發問，我是其中之一。前一晚我興奮得睡不著。那天，我們坐在學校禮堂，圍著一台大無線電。和太空站的通訊只有十分鐘，因為太空站繞著地球飛得非常快。輪到我的時候，我問：「你最想念地球的什麼？」太空人笑著說：「下雨，還有我媽媽煮的菜！」另一個同學問太空人怎麼刷牙。她說他們用特殊的牙膏，刷完就吞下去。大家都叫：「噁～！」那是我上過最短、但也最棒的一堂課。
