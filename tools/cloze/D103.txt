## 為什麼 6 怕 7
My [[classmate|weather|sugar|fever]] Andy tells a joke every morning before class. Yesterday, he stood in front of the [[classroom|fever|sugar|weather]] and asked, "Why was six afraid of seven?" Nobody knew the [[answer|weather|fever|sugar]]. "Because seven ate nine!" he said. (Seven, eight, nine. Do you get it?) Some students laughed, and some just looked at the ceiling. At that moment, our [[math|pumpkin|fever|receipt]] teacher walked in. She had heard the whole joke. "Good one, Andy," she said. "But in your homework, you wrote 7 + 8 = 16." "See?" Andy said. "Seven ate nine, so now I'm always confused about numbers!" This time, everyone laughed, even the teacher. She gave him a red pen and said, "Fix it, and tomorrow, tell us a joke about fractions." Andy says math is now his favorite [[subject|pumpkin|fever|receipt]], because it gives him so many jokes.
> 1 my classmate Andy「我的同學 Andy」。weather、sugar、fever 都不是人，不能講笑話。
> 2 in front of the classroom「在教室前面」。fever、sugar、weather 都不是有前後的地方。
> 3 Nobody knew the answer「沒人知道答案」。weather、fever、sugar 都不能是笑話的答案。
> 4 our math teacher「數學老師」，後面講算錯的作業。pumpkin、fever、receipt 都不是科目。
> 5 his favorite subject「他最喜歡的科目」。pumpkin、fever、receipt 都不是科目。
+ afraid = 害怕的
+ ate = 吃（eat 的過去式，和 eight 同音）
+ ceiling = 天花板
+ confused = 搞混的
+ fraction = 分數
= 我的同學 Andy 每天早上上課前都會講一個笑話。昨天，他站在教室前面問：「為什麼 6 會怕 7？」沒人知道答案。「因為 7 吃了 9！」他說。（seven, eight（ate，吃）, nine，懂了嗎？）有些同學笑了，有些只是盯著天花板。這時，數學老師走了進來，她聽到了整個笑話。「講得好，Andy，」她說，「可是你的作業寫 7＋8＝16。」「你看吧？」Andy 說，「7 把 9 吃掉了，所以我現在老是搞混數字！」這次大家都笑了，連老師也笑了。她給他一支紅筆說：「訂正好，明天講一個關於分數的笑話。」Andy 說數學現在是他最喜歡的科目，因為它能給他好多笑話。

## 法國的首都是 F
In [[geography|pumpkin|fever|receipt]] class, our teacher asked, "Kevin, what's the capital of France?" Kevin was half asleep. He looked at the word "France" on the board and said, "F!" My [[classmates|weathers|sugars|fevers]] couldn't stop laughing. "I asked about the capital city, not the capital [[letter|weather|fever|sugar]]," the teacher said. "The answer is Paris." Kevin's face turned red, but then he smiled. "Teacher, I have a question for you, too. What has cities but no houses, rivers but no water, and forests but no trees?" Nobody knew the [[answer|weather|fever|sugar]]. "A map!" Kevin said. Our teacher laughed. "OK, that's a good one. You can sleep less and tell more jokes in my class." After that day, Kevin started bringing a new map riddle to every lesson. Geography became the most fun [[subject|pumpkin|fever|receipt]] in our class.
> 1 geography class「地理課」，問法國首都。pumpkin、fever、receipt 都不是科目。
> 2 My classmates couldn't stop laughing「同學們笑個不停」。weathers、sugars、fevers 都不是人，不會笑。
> 3 the capital letter「大寫字母」，和 capital city（首都）是雙關。weather、fever、sugar 都不能和 capital 組成「大寫字母」。
> 4 Nobody knew the answer「沒人知道答案」。weather、fever、sugar 都不能是謎題的答案。
> 5 the most fun subject「最好玩的科目」。pumpkin、fever、receipt 都不是科目。
+ capital = 首都；大寫的（雙關）
+ France = 法國
+ Paris = 巴黎
+ forest = 森林
+ riddle = 謎語
= 地理課上，老師問：「Kevin，法國的首都是什麼？」Kevin 半睡半醒，看著黑板上的「France」說：「F！」同學們笑個不停。「我問的是首都（capital city），不是大寫字母（capital letter）。」老師說，「答案是巴黎。」Kevin 的臉都紅了，但接著他笑了：「老師，我也有一個問題問你。什麼東西有城市卻沒有房子，有河流卻沒有水，有森林卻沒有樹？」沒人知道答案。「地圖！」Kevin 說。老師笑了：「好，這題不錯。在我的課上，你可以少睡一點、多講點笑話。」從那天起，Kevin 每堂課都帶一個新的地圖謎語來。地理成了我們班最好玩的科目。
