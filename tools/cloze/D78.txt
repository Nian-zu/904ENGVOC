## 南海海槽的第一次「巨大地震注意」
On August 8, 2024, a magnitude 7.1 [[earthquake|sandwich|picnic|holiday]] hit off the coast of Miyazaki in southern Japan. That evening, the Japan Meteorological Agency did something it had never done before. It gave the country's first "megaquake advisory" for the Nankai Trough, an area under the sea where very large earthquakes have happened in the past. The advisory did not mean a big quake would surely come. It only meant the chance was higher than usual, so people should get ready. It was a [[warm|polite|honest|shy]] summer week, and many families went to the [[supermarket|dentist|blackboard|eraser]] to buy bottled water, rice, and batteries. The government asked people to stay [[cool|thirsty|hungry|sleepy]] and to check their emergency bags. On August 15, the advisory ended. My Japanese pen pal said the week taught her family to talk about their plan before the next [[earthquake|pencil|towel|spoon]] comes.
> 1 a magnitude 7.1 earthquake「一場規模 7.1 的地震」，規模是用來描述地震大小的。sandwich（三明治）、picnic（野餐）、holiday（假日）都不會有「規模」，也不會「襲擊」海岸。
> 2 a warm summer week「溫暖（炎熱）的夏季一週」，warm 形容天氣。polite（有禮貌的）、honest（誠實的）、shy（害羞的）都是形容人的個性，不能形容一週的天氣。
> 3 went to the supermarket to buy bottled water「去超市買瓶裝水」，超市是買東西的地方。dentist（牙醫）、blackboard（黑板）、eraser（橡皮擦）都不是買水和米的地方。
> 4 stay cool「保持冷靜」，cool 也可以指「沉著、不慌張」，政府請大家不要慌張。thirsty（口渴的）、hungry（餓的）、sleepy（想睡的）都不會是政府要大家「保持」的狀態。
> 5 before the next earthquake comes「在下一次地震來臨之前」，全文在談準備應付地震。pencil（鉛筆）、towel（毛巾）、spoon（湯匙）都不會「來臨」，和防災計畫無關。
+ Japan Meteorological Agency = 日本氣象廳
+ megaquake advisory = 巨大地震注意（警示）
+ Nankai Trough = 南海海槽
+ magnitude = 規模（地震大小）
+ Miyazaki = 宮崎（日本縣名）
= 2024 年 8 月 8 日，一場規模 7.1 的地震襲擊日本南部宮崎外海。那天晚上，日本氣象廳做了一件以前從沒做過的事：第一次針對南海海槽發布「巨大地震注意」。南海海槽是海底的一個區域，過去曾發生過非常大的地震。這個警示並不代表大地震一定會來，只是表示機率比平常高，所以大家應該做好準備。那是炎熱的夏季一週，很多家庭到超市買瓶裝水、米和電池。政府請民眾保持冷靜，並檢查自己的緊急避難包。8 月 15 日，警示解除了。我的日本筆友說，這一週讓她們家學會在下一次地震來臨前先討論好應變計畫。

## 富士山遲到的白雪帽

## 只剩兩個季節的日本？

## 夏天不是因為離太陽比較近
Many people think we have summer because Earth is closer to the sun. Actually, that is not true. Earth is closest to the sun in early January, when it is winter in Taiwan! The real reason for the [[seasons|pencils|spoons|towels]] is that Earth is tilted about 23.5 degrees. When the northern half of Earth leans toward the sun, sunlight hits it more directly, and the days are longer. That is why June and July are so hot in Taiwan. When it leans away, the sunlight is weaker, and the weather turns [[cool|honest|polite|famous]]. In between, we have [[spring|homework|garbage|medicine]] and [[autumn|luggage|furniture|laundry]]. In Australia, the seasons are the other way around. When we wear sweaters in December, kids in Sydney enjoy [[warm|shy|lazy|rude]] days at the beach.
> 1 The real reason for the seasons「四季真正的原因」，全文在解釋季節怎麼來的。pencils（鉛筆）、spoons（湯匙）、towels（毛巾）和地球傾斜無關。
> 2 the weather turns cool「天氣轉涼」，陽光變弱時天氣變涼。honest（誠實的）、polite（有禮貌的）、famous（有名的）都不能形容天氣變化。
> 3 In between, we have spring「在（冬夏）之間，有春天」。homework（作業）、garbage（垃圾）、medicine（藥）都不是介於冬夏之間的季節。
> 4 and autumn「和秋天」，春天和秋天都是介於冬夏之間的季節。luggage（行李）、furniture（家具）、laundry（待洗衣物）都不是季節。
> 5 enjoy warm days at the beach「在海灘享受溫暖的日子」，十二月是澳洲的夏天。shy（害羞的）、lazy（懶惰的）、rude（無禮的）都是形容人，不能形容日子的天氣。
+ tilted = 傾斜的
+ degree = 度
+ lean = 傾斜、靠向
+ directly = 直接地
+ Sydney = 雪梨
= 很多人以為我們有夏天，是因為地球離太陽比較近。其實這不是真的。地球在一月初離太陽最近，那時台灣是冬天！四季真正的原因是地球傾斜了大約 23.5 度。當地球的北半部朝向太陽時，陽光照得比較直，白天也比較長，所以台灣六、七月才會那麼熱。當北半部偏離太陽時，陽光變弱，天氣就轉涼。在這兩者之間，就是春天和秋天。在澳洲，季節剛好相反。當我們十二月穿著毛衣時，雪梨的小孩正在海灘享受溫暖的日子。

## 秋天的葉子為什麼會變色
In [[autumn|kitchen|pocket|bottle]], the leaves of many trees turn yellow, orange, and red. Many people think the trees are "painting" new colors. Actually, most of the yellow and orange colors were in the leaves all summer. Leaves look green because of chlorophyll, which helps trees make food from sunlight. When the days get shorter and the nights get [[cooler|hungrier|shyer|lazier]], trees stop making chlorophyll. As the green slowly goes away, the yellow and orange can finally be seen. Some red colors, however, are newly made in autumn. Sunny days and [[cool|honest|brave|polite]] nights make the reds brighter. In Taiwan, the weather stays [[warm|lazy|rude|careless]] for a long time, so we don't see as many red leaves as people in Japan or Canada. But in late autumn, you can find beautiful maple trees at Fushoushan Farm. My family plans to [[travel|borrow|repeat|marry]] there next year.
> 1 In autumn「在秋天」，葉子變色的季節。kitchen（廚房）、pocket（口袋）、bottle（瓶子）都不是時間，樹葉也不會在口袋裡變色。
> 2 the nights get cooler「夜晚變得更涼」，cooler 是 cool 的比較級。hungrier（更餓）、shyer（更害羞）、lazier（更懶）都是形容人或動物，不能形容夜晚。
> 3 cool nights「涼爽的夜晚」讓紅色更鮮豔。honest（誠實的）、brave（勇敢的）、polite（有禮貌的）都不能形容夜晚。
> 4 the weather stays warm「天氣一直很溫暖」，所以台灣紅葉比較少。lazy、rude、careless 都是形容人的個性，不能形容天氣。
> 5 travel there「去那裡旅行」。borrow（借入）、repeat（重複）、marry（結婚）都不能直接接 there 表示「去那裡」。
+ chlorophyll = 葉綠素
+ maple = 楓樹
