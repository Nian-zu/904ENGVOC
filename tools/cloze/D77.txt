## 聖母峰登山費漲到一萬五千美元
Mount Everest is 8,849 meters above [[sea|sofa|pencil|kitchen]] level, and every spring, hundreds of people try to climb it. In 2025, Nepal decided to raise the price of a climbing permit. Starting in September 2025, a foreign climber who wants to go up the [[mountain|wallet|soup|ticket]] from the Nepal side in spring has to pay 15,000 US dollars, instead of 11,000 dollars. It was the first big change in about ten years. The government said the money would help keep the mountain clean and safe. Many climbers leave trash on the way up, so cleaning the [[mountain|salad|sneaker|pocket]] is a big job. Climbers also have to bring their garbage back down. My uncle loves hiking, but he only climbs small [[hills|forks|spoons|socks]] near Taipei. He laughed and said, "For 15,000 dollars, I can climb Elephant Mountain every day for the rest of my life!" Then he showed me a photo of Everest and said that even if we never go there, we can still [[protect|borrow|repeat|marry]] the mountains near our home.
> 1 above sea level「海拔（高於海平面）」，sea level 是固定說法。sofa（沙發）、pencil（鉛筆）、kitchen（廚房）都不能和 level 組成「海平面」。
> 2 go up the mountain「爬上山」，後面說從尼泊爾那一側。wallet（錢包）、soup（湯）、ticket（票）都不能「爬上去」。
> 3 cleaning the mountain「清理這座山」，因為登山客沿路留下垃圾。salad（沙拉）、sneaker（運動鞋）、pocket（口袋）不會是「一件大工程」要清理的地方。
> 4 climbs small hills「爬小山丘」，和高聳的聖母峰對比。forks（叉子）、spoons（湯匙）、socks（襪子）都不能「爬」。
> 5 protect the mountains「保護山」，呼應前面要保持山的乾淨。borrow（借入）、repeat（重複）、marry（結婚）接 the mountains 都說不通。
+ Everest = 聖母峰
+ Nepal = 尼泊爾
+ permit = 許可證
+ Elephant Mountain = 象山
= 聖母峰海拔 8,849 公尺，每年春天都有好幾百人想爬上去。2025 年，尼泊爾決定調漲登山許可證的價格。從 2025 年 9 月開始，外國登山客如果想在春季從尼泊爾那一側登山，要付一萬五千美元，而不是一萬一千美元。這是大約十年來第一次大調整。政府說這筆錢會用來維持山上的乾淨和安全。很多登山客在上山途中留下垃圾，所以清理這座山是一件大工程。登山客也必須把自己的垃圾帶下山。我舅舅很喜歡健行，但他只爬台北附近的小山丘。他笑著說：「付一萬五千美元，我可以下半輩子每天都去爬象山！」接著他給我看一張聖母峰的照片，說即使我們永遠不會去那裡，還是可以保護家附近的山。

## 公海條約開始生效
About two-thirds of the world's ocean belongs to no country. These waters are called the high seas. For a long time, there were few rules to protect the fish, whales, and plants that live there. In 2023, countries at the United Nations agreed on the High Seas Treaty. In September 2025, the 60th country approved it, and the treaty came into force on January 17, 2026. Now countries can work together to create protected areas in the open [[sea|garden|kitchen|library]], where fishing and other activities are limited. Scientists say this will help sea life recover. Much of the plastic in the ocean comes from land. It is carried by [[rivers|towels|spoons|pillows]] all the way to the [[sea|shelf|drawer|sofa]]. So even people who live far from the coast, near a [[lake|cough|fever|noise]] or in the [[mountains|pockets|bottles|drawers]], can help by using less plastic. Our class now brings our own bottles to school every day.
> 1 in the open sea「在公海、遠洋上」，前面講的是海洋保護區。garden（花園）、kitchen（廚房）、library（圖書館）裡不會有捕魚活動。
> 2 carried by rivers「被河流帶走」，河流把陸地上的塑膠帶進海裡。towels（毛巾）、spoons（湯匙）、pillows（枕頭）不能把垃圾一路運到大海。
> 3 all the way to the sea「一路到海裡」。shelf（架子）、drawer（抽屜）、sofa（沙發）都不是河流的終點。
> 4 near a lake「住在湖邊」，和 far from the coast 一樣是在說住的地方。cough（咳嗽）、fever（發燒）、noise（噪音）都不是地點。
> 5 in the mountains「住在山區」。pockets（口袋）、bottles（瓶子）、drawers（抽屜）都不能住人。
+ high seas = 公海
+ treaty = 條約
+ United Nations = 聯合國
+ come into force = 生效
+ protected area = 保護區
= 世界上大約三分之二的海洋不屬於任何國家，這些海域叫做公海。很長一段時間，幾乎沒有規定來保護住在那裡的魚、鯨魚和植物。2023 年，各國在聯合國通過了《公海條約》。2025 年 9 月，第 60 個國家批准了這個條約，它在 2026 年 1 月 17 日正式生效。現在各國可以一起在遠洋上設立保護區，限制捕魚和其他活動。科學家說這會幫助海洋生物恢復。海洋裡很多塑膠來自陸地，被河流一路帶到海裡。所以就算住在離海岸很遠的地方，像是湖邊或山區，也可以少用塑膠來幫忙。我們班現在每天都自己帶水壺上學。

## 碎成上千塊的超級冰山
The word "iceberg" comes from Dutch, and "berg" means [[mountain|pillow|button|ladder]]. So an iceberg is really an "ice mountain." In 1986, a huge iceberg called A23a broke off from Antarctica. It was more than twice the size of London, but it soon got stuck on the floor of the [[sea|kitchen|garden|bedroom]] and did not move for over 30 years. In 2020, it finally began to float north. In March 2025, it ran aground again near South Georgia, an island where many penguins and seals live. Scientists worried that it might block the animals' way to food in the [[sea|closet|hallway|attic]], but the iceberg soon started to move again. By late 2025, warmer water had broken it into thousands of smaller pieces. Some of them were still as big as a small [[hill|button|spoon|crayon]]. My science teacher showed us satellite photos. In one picture, the white pieces looked like islands sitting on a dark blue [[lake|spoon|crayon|button]], but she reminded us that it was really the cold Southern Ocean.
> 1 berg means mountain「berg 的意思是山」，所以 iceberg 是「冰山」。pillow（枕頭）、button（鈕扣）、ladder（梯子）都不是 iceberg 的字義。
> 2 the floor of the sea「海底」，冰山卡在海底不動。kitchen（廚房）、garden（花園）、bedroom（臥室）的地板都不會卡住冰山。
> 3 food in the sea「海裡的食物」，企鵝和海豹在海中覓食。closet（衣櫃）、hallway（走廊）、attic（閣樓）都不是企鵝找食物的地方。
> 4 as big as a small hill「像小山丘一樣大」。button（鈕扣）、spoon（湯匙）、crayon（蠟筆）都很小，用來形容碎塊「仍然很大」不合理。
> 5 sitting on a dark blue lake「浮在深藍色的湖上」，照片看起來像湖面，老師提醒其實是海。spoon（湯匙）、crayon（蠟筆）、button（鈕扣）都太小，也不是可以浮著島嶼的水面。
+ iceberg = 冰山
+ Antarctica = 南極洲
+ run aground = 擱淺
+ South Georgia = 南喬治亞島
+ Southern Ocean = 南冰洋
= 「iceberg」這個字來自荷蘭文，「berg」的意思是山，所以冰山其實就是「冰做的山」。1986 年，一座叫 A23a 的巨大冰山從南極洲斷裂。它的面積是倫敦的兩倍多，但很快就卡在海底，三十多年都沒有移動。2020 年，它終於開始往北漂。2025 年 3 月，它又在南喬治亞島附近擱淺，那座島上住著很多企鵝和海豹。科學家擔心它會擋住動物去海裡覓食的路，但冰山不久又開始移動了。到了 2025 年底，比較溫暖的海水已經把它分裂成好幾千塊小冰山，其中有些還是像小山丘一樣大。自然老師給我們看衛星照片。在其中一張照片裡，白色的碎塊看起來像浮在深藍色湖面上的小島，但老師提醒我們，那其實是寒冷的南冰洋。

## 東京的富士山噴發 AI 影片
Mount Fuji is about 100 kilometers from Tokyo. It is a volcano, and the last time it erupted was in 1707. In August 2025, the Tokyo government shared an AI-made video to help people get ready in case it erupts again. In the video, the sky over Tokyo turns dark, and gray ash comes down from the [[mountain|cupboard|elevator|refrigerator]] and covers the streets of the city. Trains stop, and cars cannot drive well. The video tells people to keep food and water at home for about two weeks. Scientists say ash could reach Tokyo in just a few hours. The government made the video to help people prepare, not to scare them. In Taiwan, we also have volcanoes, like the [[mountains|blankets|pillows|sofas]] in Yangmingshan, but they are quiet now. After watching the video, my family checked our emergency bag. We also talked about where to go if a typhoon makes the [[river|ceiling|carpet|bookshelf]] near our home rise. If it floods, we will walk up the small [[hill|drawer|button|sock]] behind our school. My little brother said our dog's food should go in the bag, too, and he was right. Being ready keeps the whole family [[safe|salty|sour|cloudy]].
> 1 comes down from the mountain「從山上落下」，火山灰從富士山飄下來。cupboard（櫥櫃）、elevator（電梯）、refrigerator（冰箱）都不會噴出火山灰。
> 2 the mountains in Yangmingshan「陽明山的山」，大屯火山群是火山。blankets（毯子）、pillows（枕頭）、sofas（沙發）都不是火山。
> 3 makes the river rise「讓河水上漲」，颱風帶來大雨會讓河水暴漲。ceiling（天花板）、carpet（地毯）、bookshelf（書架）都不會因為颱風「上漲」淹水。
> 4 walk up the small hill「走上小山丘」，淹水時往高處走。drawer（抽屜）、button（鈕扣）、sock（襪子）都不能「走上去」避難。
> 5 keeps the whole family safe「讓全家平安」，keep + 受詞 + 形容詞。salty（鹹的）、sour（酸的）、cloudy（多雲的）都不是做好準備帶來的結果。
+ volcano = 火山
+ erupt = 噴發
+ ash = 灰（火山灰）
+ flood = 淹水
= 富士山距離東京大約 100 公里。它是一座火山，上一次噴發是在 1707 年。2025 年 8 月，東京都政府公布了一段用 AI 製作的影片，幫助大家為它可能再次噴發做準備。影片中，東京的天空變暗，灰色的火山灰從山上飄落，蓋滿了城市的街道。火車停駛，汽車也很難行駛。影片提醒大家在家裡準備大約兩週的食物和水。科學家說，火山灰可能只要幾個小時就會到東京。政府做這支影片是為了幫助大家做準備，不是要嚇人。台灣也有火山，像是陽明山的山，不過它們現在很安靜。看完影片後，我們家檢查了緊急避難包，也討論如果颱風讓家附近的河水上漲要去哪裡。如果淹水，我們就走上學校後面的小山丘。我弟弟說狗狗的飼料也要放進避難包，他說得對。做好準備，才能讓全家平安。

## 吐瓦魯人的氣候簽證
Tuvalu is a small country in the Pacific Ocean. It is made of nine low islands, and there are no [[mountains|blankets|pillows|towels]] or tall hills at all. Most of the land is only about two meters above the [[sea|sofa|shelf|carpet]]. As sea levels rise, scientists worry that much of the land could be under water during high tides in the future. In 2023, Tuvalu and Australia signed an agreement called the Falepili Union. Under it, up to 280 people from Tuvalu can move to Australia each year to live, work, and study. In June 2025, when the first chance to apply opened, more than one-third of Tuvalu's people signed up. The country has no [[rivers|forks|socks|buttons]] or lakes, so people collect rainwater to drink. Our geography teacher showed us photos of children playing football on the runway by the [[sea|closet|drawer|ceiling]]. She asked us to think about what we would miss most if we had to leave our home. I said I would miss the [[hill|button|spoon|crayon]] behind my grandma's house, where I fly kites every summer.
> 1 there are no mountains「沒有山」，吐瓦魯是低平的珊瑚島。blankets（毯子）、pillows（枕頭）、towels（毛巾）放進去和 tall hills 並列描述地形不通。
> 2 two meters above the sea「比海面高約兩公尺」。sofa（沙發）、shelf（架子）、carpet（地毯）都不是用來量陸地高度的標準。
> 3 no rivers or lakes「沒有河流或湖泊」，所以要收集雨水來喝。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、buttons（鈕扣）和「沒有水源」無關。
> 4 the runway by the sea「海邊的跑道」。closet（衣櫃）、drawer（抽屜）、ceiling（天花板）旁邊不會有飛機跑道。
> 5 the hill behind my grandma's house「奶奶家後面的山丘」，可以去放風箏。button、spoon、crayon 都太小，不能在上面放風箏。
+ Tuvalu = 吐瓦魯
+ Pacific Ocean = 太平洋
+ high tide = 滿潮
+ agreement = 協議
+ runway = 跑道
= 吐瓦魯是太平洋上的一個小國家，由九個低平的島嶼組成，完全沒有山，也沒有高的山丘。大部分的土地只比海面高大約兩公尺。隨著海平面上升，科學家擔心將來滿潮時很多土地可能會被水淹沒。2023 年，吐瓦魯和澳洲簽了一份叫「法雷皮利聯盟」的協議。根據協議，每年最多有 280 位吐瓦魯人可以移居澳洲生活、工作和讀書。2025 年 6 月第一次開放申請時，超過三分之一的吐瓦魯人報名。這個國家沒有河流也沒有湖泊，所以大家收集雨水來喝。我們的地理老師給我們看孩子們在海邊跑道上踢足球的照片。她要我們想一想，如果必須離開家鄉，我們最會想念什麼。我說我會想念奶奶家後面的山丘，我每年夏天都在那裡放風箏。

## 從太空量河流和湖泊的衛星
How much water is in the world's rivers and lakes? For a long time, scientists could only measure some of them by hand. In December 2022, NASA and France's space agency sent a satellite called SWOT into space. Its name stands for "Surface Water and Ocean Topography." SWOT can measure the height of the water in [[rivers|pockets|sneakers|wallets]], lakes, and the [[sea|closet|attic|hallway]] from about 890 kilometers above Earth. It covers almost the whole planet about every 21 days. With this information, scientists can see how much water a [[lake|towel|ladder|candle]] gains after heavy rain or loses during a dry summer. They can also study floods and how melting snow from the [[mountains|cupboards|ceilings|carpets]] fills rivers in spring. My cousin studies geography in college. She said SWOT gives the first global survey of Earth's surface water. Now, when the water in a river near a city starts to rise, people can [[prepare|bake|spell|sneeze]] earlier.
> 1 in rivers, lakes, and the sea「河流、湖泊和海洋裡的水」。pockets（口袋）、sneakers（運動鞋）、wallets（錢包）不需要從太空測量水位。
> 2 the sea「海洋」，和 rivers、lakes 並列，衛星名字裡也有 Ocean。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、hallway（走廊）都不是地表水。
> 3 how much water a lake gains「一座湖增加多少水」，下大雨後湖水變多。towel（毛巾）、ladder（梯子）、candle（蠟燭）不是衛星觀測的水體。
> 4 melting snow from the mountains「山上融化的雪」流進河裡。cupboards（櫥櫃）、ceilings（天花板）、carpets（地毯）上不會有積雪融化。
> 5 prepare earlier「更早做準備」，河水上漲時可以提早防災。bake（烤）、spell（拼字）、sneeze（打噴嚏）都和河水上漲的防災無關。
+ satellite = 衛星
+ NASA = 美國太空總署
+ topography = 地形
+ survey = 調查
+ flood = 洪水
= 世界上的河流和湖泊裡有多少水呢？很長一段時間，科學家只能用人工測量其中一部分。2022 年 12 月，美國太空總署和法國太空署把一顆叫 SWOT 的衛星送上太空。它的名字是「地表水與海洋地形」的縮寫。SWOT 可以從地球上方大約 890 公里的地方，測量河流、湖泊和海洋的水位高度。它大約每 21 天就能掃過幾乎整個地球。有了這些資料，科學家可以知道一座湖在大雨後增加多少水，或在乾燥的夏天少了多少水。他們也可以研究洪水，以及春天山上融雪怎麼注滿河流。我表姊在大學讀地理。她說 SWOT 提供了第一份全球地表水的調查。現在，當城市附近的河水開始上漲時，大家就能更早做準備。

## 聖母峰頂上的海底化石
The top of Mount Everest is the highest place on Earth. But scientists have found something surprising there: fossils of tiny animals that once lived in the [[sea|closet|attic|drawer]]. How did they get to the top of the world's highest [[mountain|carpet|pillow|ladder]]? Hundreds of millions of years ago, the rock at the top of Everest was mud at the bottom of an ancient ocean. Small sea animals died, and their shells became part of the rock. Later, the land of India moved north and pushed into Asia. The push was so strong that the old sea floor was slowly lifted up, and the Himalayas were born. This is still happening today. The mountains are still rising a few millimeters each year. In Taiwan, you can also find shell fossils on some [[hills|forks|socks|wallets]] far from the beach. Our science teacher says that if you ever see a shell on a mountain, it may be telling you that the place was once under the [[sea|sofa|ceiling|cupboard]]. Rocks, she said, are like a history [[textbook|fever|cough|noise]] of the Earth.
> 1 animals that once lived in the sea「曾經住在海裡的動物」，後面說是古老海洋的貝殼。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、drawer（抽屜）不會住著變成化石的小動物。
> 2 the world's highest mountain「世界最高的山」，指聖母峰。carpet（地毯）、pillow（枕頭）、ladder（梯子）都不是聖母峰。
> 3 on some hills far from the beach「在離海很遠的山丘上」。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、wallets（錢包）上面不會找到化石。
> 4 was once under the sea「曾經在海底」，山上的貝殼代表那裡以前是海。sofa（沙發）、ceiling（天花板）、cupboard（櫥櫃）都和地球歷史無關。
> 5 a history textbook of the Earth「地球的歷史課本」，岩石記錄了地球過去的事。fever（發燒）、cough（咳嗽）、noise（噪音）都不能記錄歷史。
+ fossil = 化石
+ ancient = 古老的
+ shell = 貝殼
+ Himalayas = 喜馬拉雅山脈
+ millimeter = 公釐
= 聖母峰頂是地球上最高的地方。但是科學家在那裡發現了令人驚訝的東西：曾經住在海裡的小動物化石。牠們是怎麼跑到世界最高的山頂上的呢？好幾億年前，聖母峰頂的岩石原本是古老海洋底部的泥巴。小小的海洋動物死後，牠們的殼就成了岩石的一部分。後來，印度這塊陸地往北移動，撞進亞洲。撞擊的力量非常大，古老的海底慢慢被抬高，喜馬拉雅山脈就誕生了。這件事現在還在發生，這些山每年還在長高幾公釐。在台灣，你也可以在一些離海很遠的山丘上找到貝殼化石。自然老師說，如果你在山上看到貝殼，它可能在告訴你那裡以前在海底。她說，岩石就像一本地球的歷史課本。

## 世界最深的貝加爾湖
Lake Baikal in Siberia, Russia, is the deepest [[lake|towel|ladder|candle]] in the world. At its deepest point, it is about 1,640 meters deep. You could put Taipei 101 into it more than three times and still not reach the bottom! It is also one of the oldest lakes on Earth, about 25 million years old. Baikal holds about 20 percent of the world's unfrozen fresh water, more than all of North America's Great Lakes together. More than 300 [[rivers|pillows|buttons|crayons]] flow into it, but only one, the Angara River, flows out. Tall [[mountains|cupboards|carpets|blankets]] stand around the lake. In winter, the lake freezes, and the ice can be over one meter thick. The ice is so clear that you can see into the water below. Baikal is also home to the nerpa, one of the only seals in the world that lives only in fresh water, not in the [[sea|closet|hallway|attic]]. My dream is to stand on the frozen lake one day and [[take|borrow|marry|repeat]] a photo of the blue ice.

## 死海其實是一座湖

## 比聖母峰還深的馬里亞納海溝

## 鮭魚靠鼻子找到回家的河

They live in the ocean for several years and travel hundreds or even thousands of kilometers. Then something amazing happens. When they are ready to lay eggs, most salmon swim back to the same stream where they were born. How do they find it? Scientists believe they use the Earth's magnetic field to find the right part of the coast. Then they use their noses. Every river has its own special smell, and young salmon remember it. On the way home, salmon swim against strong water and even jump up small waterfalls. Some of them swim up into the [[mountains|cupboards|sofas|ladders]] or into a [[lake|towel|fork|wallet]] near where they hatched. After they lay their eggs, many of them die. In Taiwan, we have a special salmon too, the Formosan landlocked salmon. It lives only in cold mountain streams and never goes to the sea. My class learned that it is very [[special|salty|cloudy|sour]] to Taiwan.

> 2 swim down to the sea「往下游游到海裡」，後面說牠們在海洋生活幾年。closet（衣櫃）、ceiling（天花板）、drawer（抽屜）都不能游進去。
> 3 swim up into the mountains「往上游游進山裡」，回到高山上的出生地。cupboards（櫥櫃）、sofas（沙發）、ladders（梯子）都不是河流流經的地方。

> 5 it is very special to Taiwan「對台灣來說非常特別」，櫻花鉤吻鮭是台灣特有的國寶魚。salty（鹹的）、cloudy（多雲的）、sour（酸的）都不能形容一種魚對台灣的意義。

## 湖水為什麼從上面開始結冰？
In very cold places, a [[lake|pocket|button|crayon]] freezes in winter. But it freezes from the top down, not from the bottom up. Why? Most things get heavier as they get colder, and water does too, but only until about 4°C. When water gets colder than that, it becomes lighter. So the coldest water stays near the top, and that is where ice forms. Ice is lighter than water, too, so it floats. The ice on top works like a blanket. It keeps the water below from getting much colder, and the water at the bottom usually stays around 4°C. That is how fish and frogs can live through the winter in lakes and [[rivers|pillows|carpets|ladders]] that are covered with ice. If ice sank, many lakes would freeze solid, and most of the life inside them would die. Last winter, my cousin in Canada sent me a video. People were skating on a frozen lake in front of snowy [[mountains|cupboards|wallets|forks]]. Under the ice, she said, the fish were swimming slowly in the dark. I think the [[sea|closet|attic|drawer]] is too salty and too big to freeze like that, except near the North and South Poles. Our teacher said that is [[right|salty|cloudy|sour]].
> 1 a lake freezes「湖會結冰」，全文在解釋湖水結冰的方式。pocket（口袋）、button（鈕扣）、crayon（蠟筆）都不會在冬天結冰形成冰層。
> 2 lakes and rivers covered with ice「被冰覆蓋的湖和河」，魚和青蛙在冰下過冬。pillows（枕頭）、carpets（地毯）、ladders（梯子）裡不會住著魚。
> 3 in front of snowy mountains「在白雪覆蓋的山前面」。cupboards（櫥櫃）、wallets（錢包）、forks（叉子）不會有積雪、不能當背景。
> 4 the sea is too salty「海水太鹹」，鹽讓海水不容易結冰。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、drawer（抽屜）不會是「太鹹」的。
> 5 that is right「那是對的」，老師同意我的想法。salty（鹹的）、cloudy（多雲的）、sour（酸的）都不能用來評論一個想法對不對。
+ freeze = 結冰
+ float = 漂浮
+ sink = 下沉（過去式 sank）
+ solid = 固體的、整塊的
+ skate = 溜冰
= 在很冷的地方，湖到了冬天會結冰。但湖是從上往下結冰，而不是從底部往上。為什麼呢？大部分的東西越冷越重，水也是，但只到大約攝氏 4 度。水比這個溫度更冷時，反而會變輕。所以最冷的水留在上層，冰就在那裡形成。冰也比水輕，所以會浮在上面。上面的冰就像一條毯子，讓下面的水不會變得更冷，湖底的水通常維持在大約 4 度。這就是為什麼魚和青蛙能在結了冰的湖和河裡過冬。如果冰會下沉，很多湖就會整個凍住，裡面大部分的生物都會死掉。去年冬天，我在加拿大的表姊傳給我一段影片。大家在白雪覆蓋的山前面，在結冰的湖上溜冰。她說，冰面下的魚在黑暗中慢慢地游。我覺得海太鹹又太大了，除了南北極附近，都不會那樣結冰。老師說我說得對。

## 飛進海灣的全壘打
San Francisco is a city of steep [[hills|forks|socks|wallets]], and it sits next to the Pacific Ocean. The San Francisco Giants play baseball at Oracle Park, right next to the water of the bay. Behind the right field wall is a small part of the bay called McCovey Cove. When a Giants player hits a home run over that wall and the ball lands in the water, it is called a "splash hit." Since the ballpark opened in 2000, Giants players have hit many of them. Barry Bonds hit the most, 35 in all. During games, fans wait in kayaks and small boats on the water, which is usually as calm as a [[lake|towel|button|crayon]]. When a ball splashes down, they paddle as fast as they can to catch it. The water in the cove connects to the [[sea|closet|attic|drawer]], so some fans joke that a lucky ball could float all the way to Japan. My uncle lives in San Francisco. He once waited in a kayak for three hours. No ball came, but he still [[enjoyed|borrowed|repeated|spelled]] the day. He says the view of the city and the [[mountains|carpets|pillows|cupboards]] across the bay is worth it.
> 1 a city of steep hills「到處是陡峭山坡的城市」，舊金山以山坡多聞名。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、wallets（錢包）不能形容城市的地形。
> 2 as calm as a lake「像湖一樣平靜」，海灣裡的水通常很平靜。towel（毛巾）、button（鈕扣）、crayon（蠟筆）不是水面，不能拿來比喻平靜的水。
> 3 connects to the sea「和大海相通」，所以開玩笑說球會漂到日本。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、drawer（抽屜）不會和海灣的水相連。
> 4 still enjoyed the day「還是很享受那一天」，和「沒接到球」形成轉折。borrowed（借）、repeated（重複）、spelled（拼）接 the day 都說不通。
> 5 the mountains across the bay「海灣對面的山」，值得欣賞的風景。carpets（地毯）、pillows（枕頭）、cupboards（櫥櫃）不會是海灣對岸的景色。
+ bay = 海灣
+ McCovey Cove = 麥考維灣
+ splash hit = 落水全壘打
+ kayak = 獨木舟
+ paddle = 划槳
= 舊金山是一座到處是陡峭山坡的城市，就在太平洋旁邊。舊金山巨人隊的主場甲骨文球場就在海灣的水邊。右外野牆後面是海灣的一小部分，叫做麥考維灣。巨人隊球員把全壘打打過那面牆、球落進水裡時，就叫做「落水全壘打」。從球場 2000 年啟用以來，巨人隊球員已經打出很多支。貝瑞‧邦茲打最多，一共 35 支。比賽時，球迷坐在獨木舟和小船上在水面等待，那裡的水通常像湖一樣平靜。球一落水，他們就拚命划過去搶。灣裡的水和大海相通，所以有些球迷開玩笑說，幸運的球說不定會一路漂到日本。我舅舅住在舊金山。他曾經坐在獨木舟上等了三個小時，雖然一顆球也沒有來，但他還是很享受那一天。他說，看看城市和海灣對面的山，就很值得了。

## 爬上聖母峰 31 次的雪巴人
On May 27, 2025, Kami Rita Sherpa reached the top of Mount Everest for the 31st time. No one in history has climbed the world's highest [[mountain|carpet|pillow|ladder]] more times. Kami Rita is a Sherpa from Nepal. In the Himalayas, where he grew up, many villages are built on steep [[hills|forks|socks|buttons]]. He first reached the top of Everest in 1994, and since then, he has gone up almost every year as a guide. Guides like him carry heavy bags, fix ropes, and help other climbers stay safe. At 8,849 meters above [[sea|sofa|shelf|drawer]] level, there is very little air to breathe, and the weather can change in minutes. Many people now call him the "Everest Man." I can't even walk up the [[hill|crayon|spoon|wallet]] to my school without stopping to rest. After reading his story, I [[decided|slept|sneezed|arrived]] to walk up it every morning instead of taking the bus.
> 1 the world's highest mountain「世界最高的山」，指聖母峰。carpet（地毯）、pillow（枕頭）、ladder（梯子）都不是聖母峰。
> 2 built on steep hills「建在陡峭的山坡上」。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、buttons（鈕扣）上面不能蓋村莊。

+ Nepal = 尼泊爾

= 2025 年 5 月 27 日，卡米‧瑞塔‧雪巴第 31 次登上聖母峰頂。歷史上沒有人爬上這座世界最高的山比他更多次。卡米‧瑞塔是來自尼泊爾的雪巴人。在他長大的喜馬拉雅山區，很多村莊都建在陡峭的山坡上。他在 1994 年第一次登頂聖母峰，從那時起幾乎每年都以嚮導的身分上山。像他這樣的嚮導要背很重的行李、架設繩索，幫助其他登山者保持安全。在海拔 8,849 公尺的地方，空氣非常稀薄，天氣也可能在幾分鐘內就改變。很多人現在叫他「聖母峰先生」。我連走上去學校的那段山坡都要停下來休息。讀了他的故事後，我決定每天早上改成走上坡去學校，不搭公車了。

## 箱根驛傳的「山神」
Every year on January 2 and 3, university teams in Japan run the Hakone Ekiden, a relay race from Tokyo to Lake Ashi in Hakone and back. Each team has ten runners, and together they run about 217 kilometers. The race goes along the [[sea|attic|closet|drawer]] for part of the way, and many families watch it on TV during the New Year holiday. The most difficult part is the fifth leg. The runner has to climb a long road up the [[mountain|pillow|carpet|button]], going up more than 800 meters, before running down to the [[lake|towel|crayon|ladder]]. A runner who is very good on this part is often called the "god of the mountain." In 2026, Asahi Kuroda of Aoyama Gakuin University ran the fifth leg faster than anyone ever had, and his team went on to win the race. My cousin in Japan said her whole family cheered in front of the TV. Last month, our school had a relay race. I asked to run the part that goes up the [[hill|fork|sock|wallet]] behind the playground. I didn't become a god, but my classmates still [[cheered|sneezed|slept|coughed]] for me all the way to the top!
> 1 goes along the sea「沿著海邊」，部分路線沿海岸。attic（閣樓）、closet（衣櫃）、drawer（抽屜）都不能讓馬路沿著走。
> 2 a long road up the mountain「一段上山的長路」，第五區要爬升八百多公尺。pillow（枕頭）、carpet（地毯）、button（鈕扣）都不能「往上爬」八百公尺。
> 3 running down to the lake「往下跑到湖邊」，終點在蘆之湖。towel（毛巾）、crayon（蠟筆）、ladder（梯子）不是賽跑的終點地點。
> 4 the hill behind the playground「操場後面的山坡」，呼應「山神」爬坡。fork（叉子）、sock（襪子）、wallet（錢包）都不能跑上去。
> 5 cheered for me「為我加油」，cheer for 是固定搭配。sneezed（打噴嚏）、slept（睡覺）、coughed（咳嗽）都不能接 for me 一路陪我到坡頂。
+ Hakone Ekiden = 箱根驛傳
+ relay race = 接力賽
+ Lake Ashi = 蘆之湖
+ leg = （接力賽的）一段、區間
+ Aoyama Gakuin University = 青山學院大學
= 每年 1 月 2 日和 3 日，日本各大學的隊伍會參加箱根驛傳，這是一場從東京跑到箱根蘆之湖再跑回來的接力賽。每隊有十位跑者，一共大約跑 217 公里。部分路線沿著海邊，很多家庭會在新年假期看電視轉播。最困難的是第五區。跑者要沿著一條長長的山路往上爬，爬升八百多公尺，然後再往下跑到湖邊。很會跑這一段的選手常被稱為「山神」。2026 年，青山學院大學的黑田朝日跑出史上最快的第五區成績，他的隊伍最後也贏得冠軍。我在日本的表姊說，她全家都在電視前加油。上個月，我們學校舉辦接力賽。我自願跑操場後面上坡的那一段。我沒有變成山神，但同學們還是一路幫我加油到坡頂！

## 繞著白朗峰跑 170 公里
Mont Blanc is the highest [[mountain|carpet|pillow|button]] in the Alps. Every year in late August, about 2,500 runners take part in the UTMB, a race that goes all the way around it. The course is about 170 kilometers long and passes through three countries: France, Italy, and Switzerland. Runners climb up and down about 10,000 meters, which is more than going up Mount Everest from [[sea|sofa|ceiling|closet]] level. They run day and night, through forests, along [[rivers|forks|wallets|crayons]], and over high passes. The most famous runner in this race is Kilian Jornet from Spain. He won it in 2008, 2009, and 2011. Then, in 2022, he won for the fourth time and became the first person to finish it in under 20 hours. When he was a child, he grew up in a mountain hut in the Pyrenees, and he started climbing [[mountains|cupboards|ladders|towels]] with his family when he was very young. My dad runs a 10-kilometer race every year. After he read about Kilian, he said he wants to try a trail race on the [[hills|socks|spoons|forks]] near Taipei next spring.
> 1 the highest mountain in the Alps「阿爾卑斯山最高的山」。carpet（地毯）、pillow（枕頭）、button（鈕扣）都不在阿爾卑斯山上比高度。
> 2 from sea level「從海平面算起」，sea level 是固定說法。sofa（沙發）、ceiling（天花板）、closet（衣櫃）都不能和 level 組成「海平面」。
> 3 along rivers「沿著河流」，和 through forests、over high passes 並列描述路線。forks（叉子）、wallets（錢包）、crayons（蠟筆）不是賽道經過的地形。
> 4 started climbing mountains「開始爬山」，他在庇里牛斯山長大。cupboards（櫥櫃）、ladders（梯子）、towels（毛巾）放進去都和山區長大的背景不合，也不是和家人一起的戶外活動。
> 5 a trail race on the hills「在山丘上的越野賽」。socks（襪子）、spoons（湯匙）、forks（叉子）上面不能舉辦賽跑。
+ Mont Blanc = 白朗峰
+ the Alps = 阿爾卑斯山脈
+ pass = 山口、隘口
+ hut = 小屋
+ Pyrenees = 庇里牛斯山脈
= 白朗峰是阿爾卑斯山脈最高的山。每年八月底，大約有兩千五百位跑者參加 UTMB，這是一場繞著白朗峰跑一整圈的比賽。賽道大約 170 公里長，經過法國、義大利和瑞士三個國家。跑者上上下下的總爬升大約一萬公尺，比從海平面爬上聖母峰還多。他們日夜不停地跑，穿過森林、沿著河流、翻越高山隘口。這場比賽最有名的跑者是西班牙的基利安‧霍爾內特。他在 2008、2009 和 2011 年贏得冠軍。到了 2022 年，他第四度奪冠，也成為第一個在 20 小時內跑完的人。他小時候在庇里牛斯山的一間山屋長大，很小就跟著家人一起爬山。我爸爸每年都會跑一場十公里的路跑。讀了基利安的故事後，他說明年春天想去台北附近的山丘試試越野賽。

## 李安與《斷背山》
In 2005, Taiwanese director Ang Lee made a film called Brokeback Mountain. It tells the story of two young cowboys who meet in 1963 while they are working on a [[mountain|carpet|pillow|button]] in Wyoming, in the United States. Over the summer, they become close and fall in love. At that time, very few big Hollywood films told a love story between two men. The film shows wide, quiet places: green [[hills|forks|socks|wallets]], cold [[rivers|crayons|ladders|towels]], and a deep blue [[lake|spoon|drawer|closet]] under the snowy peaks. Much of it was filmed in the Canadian Rockies. The film won the Golden Lion at the Venice Film Festival in 2005. In March 2006, Ang Lee won the Oscar for Best Director and became the first Asian director to win it. Many people in Taiwan watched the award show on TV and felt proud. Our teacher told us that the film helped many people understand that love between two men is also [[real|salty|cloudy|sour]] love. She said good stories can help us see other people's lives more clearly.
> 1 working on a mountain「在山上工作」，故事發生在懷俄明州的山上，片名也叫斷背山。carpet（地毯）、pillow（枕頭）、button（鈕扣）不能在上面放牧工作一整個夏天。
> 2 green hills「綠色的山丘」，描述電影裡的風景。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、wallets（錢包）不是寬闊安靜的自然景色。
> 3 cold rivers「冰冷的河流」。crayons（蠟筆）、ladders（梯子）、towels（毛巾）不會出現在山林風景裡，也不會是「冷的」水。
> 4 a deep blue lake「深藍色的湖」，在雪峰下。spoon（湯匙）、drawer（抽屜）、closet（衣櫃）不是自然景觀。
> 5 is also real love「也是真正的愛」。salty（鹹的）、cloudy（多雲的）、sour（酸的）都不能用來形容愛情是否真實。
+ director = 導演
+ Brokeback Mountain = 《斷背山》
+ Wyoming = 懷俄明州
+ Canadian Rockies = 加拿大落磯山脈
+ Golden Lion = 金獅獎
= 2005 年，台灣導演李安拍了一部叫《斷背山》的電影。故事講兩個年輕牛仔在 1963 年於美國懷俄明州的山上工作時相遇。那個夏天，他們越來越親近，並且相愛了。當時，很少有好萊塢大片講兩個男人之間的愛情故事。電影呈現了遼闊安靜的地方：綠色的山丘、冰冷的河流，還有雪峰下一座深藍色的湖。片中很多場景是在加拿大落磯山脈拍的。這部電影在 2005 年拿下威尼斯影展的金獅獎。2006 年 3 月，李安贏得奧斯卡最佳導演獎，成為第一位得到這個獎的亞洲導演。很多台灣人在電視前看頒獎典禮，都覺得很驕傲。老師告訴我們，這部電影讓很多人明白，兩個男人之間的愛也是真正的愛。她說，好的故事可以幫助我們更清楚地看見別人的生活。

## 裝著義肢登上聖母峰的阿魯妮瑪
Arunima Sinha from India was a national volleyball player. In 2011, she was badly hurt while traveling on a train, and doctors had to remove part of her left leg. While she was in the hospital, she made a surprising decision. She wanted to climb Mount Everest, the highest [[mountain|carpet|pillow|button]] in the world. Many people thought it was impossible. With a new leg made of metal and plastic, she started training. She practiced walking up and down steep [[hills|forks|socks|wallets]] for hours, even when her leg hurt. She also learned from Bachendri Pal, the first Indian woman to climb Everest. On May 21, 2013, Arunima reached the top, 8,849 meters above [[sea|sofa|ceiling|closet]] level. She became the first woman with an artificial leg to climb Everest. Later, she climbed the highest [[mountains|cupboards|ladders|towels]] on other continents, too. Today, she gives talks to students and helps people with disabilities play sports. My cousin, who uses a wheelchair, says Arunima taught her that a body is not the only thing that decides how high you can [[rise|cook|wash|draw]].
> 1 the highest mountain in the world「世界最高的山」，指聖母峰。carpet（地毯）、pillow（枕頭）、button（鈕扣）都不是聖母峰。
> 2 walking up and down steep hills「在陡峭的山坡上走上走下」練習。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、wallets（錢包）都不能走上去。
> 3 above sea level「海拔」，sea level 是固定說法。sofa（沙發）、ceiling（天花板）、closet（衣櫃）都不能和 level 組成「海平面」。
> 4 the highest mountains on other continents「其他各大洲的最高峰」。cupboards（櫥櫃）、ladders（梯子）、towels（毛巾）都不能「攀登」各大洲最高的。
> 5 how high you can rise「你能升到多高」，比喻能達到的成就。cook（煮）、wash（洗）、draw（畫）放進去說不通，例如 how high you can cook 沒有意義。
+ volleyball = 排球（國家隊選手）
+ remove = 切除、移除
+ artificial leg = 義肢
+ continent = 大洲
+ disability = 身心障礙
= 印度的阿魯妮瑪‧辛哈曾經是國家排球隊選手。2011 年，她在搭火車時受了重傷，醫生必須切除她左腿的一部分。住院期間，她做了一個令人驚訝的決定：她要攀登世界最高的山——聖母峰。很多人覺得這不可能。裝上金屬和塑膠做的新腿後，她開始訓練。她練習在陡峭的山坡上走上走下好幾個小時，就算腿很痛也不停。她也向第一位登上聖母峰的印度女性巴琛德莉‧帕爾學習。2013 年 5 月 21 日，阿魯妮瑪登上海拔 8,849 公尺的峰頂，成為第一位裝著義肢登上聖母峰的女性。後來，她也攀登了其他各大洲的最高峰。現在，她到學校演講，也幫助身心障礙者參與運動。我坐輪椅的表姊說，阿魯妮瑪讓她明白，身體並不是唯一能決定你能走多高的東西。

## 十八歲開始清理海洋的波揚
When Boyan Slat from the Netherlands was 16, he went diving in the [[sea|closet|attic|drawer]] in Greece. He was shocked to see more plastic bags than fish. He decided to find a way to clean the ocean. In 2013, when he was only 18, he started a group called The Ocean Cleanup. His team built long floating systems that collect plastic in the Pacific Ocean, where a huge area of trash floats between Hawaii and California. But Boyan also learned something important: most plastic gets into the ocean from [[rivers|pillows|carpets|crayons]]. So in 2019, his team showed a new machine called the Interceptor. It sits in a river and catches trash before it reaches the [[sea|sofa|shelf|ceiling]]. Today, Interceptors work in rivers in several countries. Our class watched a video about him and then went to clean up the beach near our school. We filled ten bags with bottles and plastic. Our teacher reminded us that trash from a [[lake|towel|button|ladder]] or a city street can also end up in the ocean. Now my family always [[carries|sneezes|sleeps|arrives]] our own shopping bags.
> 1 went diving in the sea「去海裡潛水」。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、drawer（抽屜）都不能潛水。
> 2 gets into the ocean from rivers「從河流進入海洋」，所以後來在河上攔截垃圾。pillows（枕頭）、carpets（地毯）、crayons（蠟筆）不會把塑膠運進海裡。
> 3 before it reaches the sea「在流到海裡之前」。sofa（沙發）、shelf（架子）、ceiling（天花板）都不是河裡垃圾的終點。
> 4 trash from a lake「湖裡的垃圾」，和 city street 一樣最後可能流進海洋。towel（毛巾）、button（鈕扣）、ladder（梯子）不是垃圾會流出來的水域或地點。
> 5 always carries our own shopping bags「總是自己帶購物袋」，主詞 my family 用單數動詞 carries。sneezes（打噴嚏）、sleeps（睡覺）、arrives（到達）都不能接 bags 當受詞。
+ Netherlands = 荷蘭
+ diving = 潛水
+ floating = 漂浮的

## 象山上的夜景
Last Friday night, my dad and I climbed Elephant Mountain in Taipei. It is not a very tall [[mountain|carpet|pillow|button]], but the stairs are steep. After about twenty minutes, my legs were tired, and I wanted to stop. "Just a little more," my dad said. When we finally reached the top of the [[hill|fork|sock|wallet]], I forgot how tired I was. Taipei 101 was right in front of us, shining with blue and white lights. Behind the city, we could see the dark shapes of the [[mountains|cupboards|ladders|towels]] around the Taipei Basin. My dad pointed to a long line of lights far away. "That's the Tamsui [[River|Crayon|Spoon|Button]]," he said. "It goes all the way to the sea." Many people were taking photos, and a group of tourists from Japan asked me to take a picture of them. I said "Cheese!" in Japanese, and they all laughed. On the way down, my dad told me that when he was in junior high, he came here with his classmates and dreamed about his future. "Now I'm here with you," he said. I think next time I will bring my best friend and [[share|borrow|repeat|marry]] this view with him.

> 2 the top of the hill「山頂、坡頂」。fork（叉子）、sock（襪子）、wallet（錢包）都沒有可以站上去看夜景的「頂端」。

> 4 the Tamsui River「淡水河」，後面說一路流到海。Crayon（蠟筆）、Spoon（湯匙）、Button（鈕扣）都不會流到海裡。
> 5 share this view with him「和他分享這片景色」，share A with B。borrow（借）、repeat（重複）、marry（結婚）都不能接 this view with him。

## 七星潭的石頭沙灘
During winter vacation, my family went to Qixingtan in Hualien. Many people think it is a [[lake|towel|button|ladder]] because of its name, but it is actually a beach by the Pacific Ocean. The beach is not covered with sand. It is covered with smooth, round stones that were carried down from the [[mountains|pillows|carpets|crayons]] by rivers. The water there is very blue, and the waves are strong, so swimming in the [[sea|closet|attic|drawer]] is not allowed. My little sister and I collected stones of different colors and lined them up on the beach. Then my dad showed us how to listen to the waves. When a wave goes back into the sea, the stones roll and make a sound like rain. Behind us, the green [[hills|forks|socks|wallets]] of the Central Mountain Range looked very close. It was a little cold, so my mom bought us hot drinks from a small shop. Before we left, my dad reminded us to put the stones back. "If everyone takes some home," he said, "the beach will [[disappear|cook|wash|bake]] one day."
> 1 Many people think it is a lake「很多人以為它是湖」，因為名字裡有「潭」。towel（毛巾）、button（鈕扣）、ladder（梯子）和名字造成的誤會無關。
> 2 carried down from the mountains by rivers「被河流從山上帶下來」。pillows（枕頭）、carpets（地毯）、crayons（蠟筆）上不會有石頭被河流帶下來。
> 3 swimming in the sea is not allowed「禁止在海裡游泳」，因為浪很大。closet（衣櫃）、attic（閣樓）、drawer（抽屜）本來就不能游泳，和浪大無關。
> 4 the green hills of the Central Mountain Range「中央山脈的綠色山丘」。forks（叉子）、socks（襪子）、wallets（錢包）不能用來描述山脈景色。
> 5 the beach will disappear「沙灘會消失」，大家都把石頭帶走的後果。cook（煮）、wash（洗）、bake（烤）都不能用來說沙灘的結果。
