## 弟弟的作業說謊記
My little brother Max hates homework. Every night, Mom asks, "Have you finished your homework?" And every night, Max answers, "Yes! I've [[already|than|whether|below]] finished it." But one night, Mom decided to check his bag. Max's notebook was empty. He had been playing games on his tablet [[since|than|whether|below]] dinner. Mom wasn't angry, but she made a new rule. Now Max [[usually|than|since|below]] does his homework at the kitchen table while Mom cooks. If he has a question, he doesn't have to [[wait|follow|travel|grow]] long for help, because Mom is right there. Max complained a lot at first. He said the kitchen was too noisy and too hot. But after a month, his grades got much better, and his teacher wrote "Great job!" on his math test. Last week, Max even said to me, "You should [[follow|wait|travel|grow]] my example and do your homework early." I just laughed.
> 1 have already + 過去分詞「已經…了」：Max 每次都說他已經寫完了。than、whether、below 不能放在 I've 和 finished 中間。
> 2 since + 過去時間點，搭配完成進行式 had been playing「從晚餐後就一直在玩」。than、whether、below dinner 意思不通。
> 3 usually「通常」放在一般動詞 does 前面，表示現在的習慣。than、since、below 不能這樣修飾動詞。
> 4 wait long for help「等很久才得到幫忙」：媽媽就在旁邊，不用等。follow、travel、grow long for help 意思不通。
> 5 follow one's example「以某人為榜樣、照著做」。wait my example、travel my example、grow my example 都不通。
+ tablet = 平板電腦
+ grade = 成績
= 我弟弟 Max 討厭寫作業。每天晚上媽媽都會問：「作業寫完了嗎？」而每天晚上 Max 都回答：「寫完了！我已經寫完了。」但有一天晚上，媽媽決定檢查他的書包，結果 Max 的作業簿是空白的。他從吃完晚餐就一直在用平板玩遊戲。媽媽沒有生氣，但訂了一條新規定。現在 Max 通常在媽媽煮飯時坐在廚房餐桌寫作業。有問題時，他不用等很久，因為媽媽就在旁邊。一開始 Max 一直抱怨，說廚房太吵又太熱。但一個月後，他的成績進步很多，老師還在他的數學考卷上寫了「做得好！」上星期，Max 甚至對我說：「你應該學我，早點寫作業。」我只能笑一笑。
