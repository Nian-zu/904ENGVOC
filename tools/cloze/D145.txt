His voice was very [[loud|wise|careful|cheap]]. "Yes, I'm at the movies. What? Say that again!" he said. He kept asking the other person to [[repeat|belong|boil|arrive]] everything. Many people turned around and looked at him, but he didn't stop. Finally, an old woman next to him said, "Excuse me, sir. This is a public place. It doesn't [[belong|repeat|type|boil]] only to you." The man's face turned red, and he put his phone away. After the movie, my friend said, "That woman was so brave." "She was also [[wise|loud|cheap|thick]]," I said. "She didn't shout. She just said the right words." I learned that a few polite words can make a big [[difference|pair|type|event]].
> 1 very loud「很大聲」，大家都轉頭看他。wise（有智慧的）、careful（小心的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能形容講電話的音量。
> 2 ask someone to repeat everything「要對方每句話都再說一次」，前面他說「再說一次」。belong 要接 to；boil、arrive 接 everything 都說不通。
> 3 belong only to you「只屬於你」，公共場所是大家的。repeat、type、boil 都不能和 to you 搭配表示屬於誰。
> 4 wise「有智慧的」，她沒有大吼，只說了對的話。loud（大聲的）和「她沒有大吼」相反；cheap（便宜的）、thick（厚的）不能稱讚人。
> 5 make a big difference「造成很大的不同」。pair、type、event 都不能和 make a big 組成這個片語。
+ superhero = 超級英雄
+ theater = 電影院
+ public = 公共的
+ sir = 先生
= 上星期天，我和朋友去看一部新的超級英雄電影。電影院坐滿了人。就在最精彩的部分之前，我們後面有一個男人開始講電話。他的聲音非常大：「對，我在看電影。什麼？再說一次！」他一直要對方每句話都再說一次。很多人轉頭看他，但他還是沒停。最後，他旁邊的一位老奶奶說：「先生，不好意思，這裡是公共場所，不是只屬於你一個人的。」那個男人臉都紅了，把手機收了起來。電影結束後，我朋友說：「那位奶奶好勇敢。」「她也很有智慧，」我說，「她沒有大吼，只是說了對的話。」我學到，幾句有禮貌的話就能帶來很大的不同。

## 阿公學注音打字
My grandpa got a smartphone for his eightieth birthday. He wanted to send messages to his grandchildren, but he didn't know how to [[type|belong|arrive|boil]]. So I taught him to use Zhuyin. At first, he was very slow. He looked at every key for a long time and was [[careful|loud|deep|cheap]] not to make mistakes. After a week, he sent me his first message: "Good night, my dear." I was so happy that I showed it to my whole class. Now Grandpa sends messages to our family group every morning. He likes to send the same [[type|pair|event|bill]] of picture: flowers with the words "Good morning." Sometimes he sends the same picture three times because he forgets. When I tell him, he just laughs and says, "Then I'm sending you three times more love." Grandma says the phone has made a big [[difference|pair|event|bill]] in his life. He doesn't feel lonely anymore. I think Grandpa is [[wise|loud|cheap|thick]]. He knows it's never too late to learn something new.
> 1 know how to type「知道怎麼打字」，才能傳訊息。belong 要接 to；arrive（到達）、boil（沸騰）都和傳訊息無關。
> 2 be careful not to…「小心不要…」，每個按鍵都看很久。loud（大聲的）、deep（深的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能和 not to make mistakes 搭配。
> 3 the same type of picture「同一種圖片」，冒號後面說是什麼圖。pair 後面要接複數名詞；event、bill 都不能和 of picture 搭配。
> 4 make a big difference in his life「讓他的生活大不相同」，他不再孤單了。pair、event、bill 都不能和 make a big ___ 搭配。
> 5 wise「有智慧的」，他知道學習永遠不嫌晚。loud（大聲的）、cheap（便宜的）、thick（厚的）都和後一句的意思接不上。
+ smartphone = 智慧型手機
+ Zhuyin = 注音
+ key = 按鍵
+ dear = 親愛的人
= 阿公八十歲生日時收到一支智慧型手機。他想傳訊息給孫子們，但他不會打字，所以我教他用注音。一開始他打得很慢，每個按鍵都看很久，很小心不要打錯。一個星期後，他傳了第一則訊息給我：「晚安，親愛的。」我開心到拿給全班看。現在阿公每天早上都在家族群組傳訊息。他喜歡傳同一種圖片：花上面寫著「早安」。有時候他會忘記，同一張圖傳三次。我跟他說的時候，他只是笑著說：「那我就是傳了三倍的愛給你們。」阿嬤說這支手機讓阿公的生活大不相同，他不再覺得孤單了。我覺得阿公很有智慧，他知道學新東西永遠不嫌晚。

## 魔法學校的大聲咒
At Willow Magic School, students learn a new spell every week. This week, Professor Moon taught us the Loud Spell. "With this spell, a soft sound becomes very [[loud|wise|careful|cheap]]," she said. "Be [[careful|thick|deep|delicious]] with it." My friend Max wasn't listening. He was drawing a dragon in his notebook. When it was his turn, he pointed his wand at a mouse and said the magic words. The mouse opened its mouth and made a tiny sound, but it was louder than thunder! Windows shook, and books fell off the shelves. Professor Moon quickly [[repeated|belonged|arrived|boiled]] the magic words backward, and the mouse was quiet again. "Max," she said, "a wand doesn't [[belong|repeat|type|boil]] in the hands of someone who doesn't listen." Max said sorry. Then he took a [[deep|national|wise|cheap]] breath and tried again. This time, he did it perfectly.
> 1 become very loud「變得很大聲」，這是「大聲咒」。wise（有智慧的）、careful（小心的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能形容聲音變大。
> 2 Be careful with it.「使用時要小心」。thick（厚的）、deep（深的）、delicious（美味的）都不能和 with it 搭配表示提醒。
> 3 repeat the words backward「把咒語倒著再念一次」，讓老鼠恢復安靜。belonged 要接 to；arrived、boiled 接 the magic words 都說不通。
> 4 belong in someone's hands「適合（屬於）某人手中」，不聽課的人不配拿魔杖。repeat、type、boil 都不能和 in the hands of 搭配。
> 5 take a deep breath「深呼吸」。national（國家的）、wise（有智慧的）、cheap（便宜的）都不能和 breath 搭配。
+ spell = 咒語
+ professor = 教授
+ wand = 魔杖
+ thunder = 雷
+ backward = 倒著；向後
= 在柳樹魔法學校，學生每個星期都要學一個新咒語。這個星期，月亮教授教我們「大聲咒」。「用了這個咒語，小小的聲音就會變得非常大，」她說，「使用時要小心。」我朋友 Max 沒在聽，他正在筆記本上畫一隻龍。輪到他的時候，他用魔杖指著一隻老鼠，念出咒語。老鼠張開嘴發出一點點聲音，卻比打雷還大聲！窗戶都在震動，書也從架子上掉了下來。月亮教授趕快把咒語倒著念一次，老鼠又安靜了下來。「Max，」她說，「不聽課的人不配拿魔杖。」Max 道了歉，然後深呼吸，再試一次。這一次，他做得完美無缺。
