## 士林夜市的糖葫蘆
On Saturday night, my best friend Amy and I went to the Shilin Night Market. There were so many people that it was very [[crowded|honest|lazy|polite]]. Amy wanted to try tanghulu, the shiny candy fruit on a stick. We found a shop that sold many kinds. There were sticks of [[strawberries|tickets|offices|uniforms]], grapes, and even small cherry [[tomatoes|letters|pockets|museums]]! I chose a tomato one because I was [[curious|wooden|cheap|thick]] about the taste. It was sweet and a little sour. Amy chose strawberry, but she bit too hard, and the candy broke into pieces. Some of them fell on her shirt, and we laughed so hard. On the way home, we bought a cup of fresh [[mango|weather|traffic|homework]] juice to share.
